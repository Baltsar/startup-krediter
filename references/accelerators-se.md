# Swedish accelerators and incubators

Accelerators trade equity, warrants or convertible instruments for money, coaching and investor access: Sting takes up to 2% in warrants per six-month block and routes a possible 600 000 SEK through Propel Capital, Antler buys 8.5% for its first 100 000 EUR, Norrsken Evolve targets 5% ownership when it leads. Incubators and pre-incubators tied to universities (KTH Innovation, Chalmers Ventures' camps, Sting's Test Drive) are free and take no ownership, but are gated on affiliation or region: KTH Innovation serves current KTH students, researchers and employees; Chalmers Ventures' Startup Camp requires a connection to Vastra Gotaland and research from a university in western Sweden; Minc requires a Malmo or Skane base. Those affiliation and region gates have no field in the matcher, so they live in the `who` text and notes, and the founder must read them before applying. Not covered here: the other regional incubators (Uppsala Innovation Centre, LEAD, Ideon, Science Park Jonkoping, BizMaker, Arctic Business and the rest of the Vinnova incubator network), corporate programs, and Nordic accelerators outside Sweden. All pages were read on 2026-10-15 unless an entry says otherwise.

## Sting Core {#sting-core}

- **Who:** Tech startups in climate, health, AI or deep tech at MVP/prototype stage (TRL 3-6), before commercial launch or recurring revenue; at least two committed co-founders working 50-100%, operational team owning 75% or more of the company.
- **What:** Flagship program of up to 18 months in three 6-month blocks (5 months active support, 1 month wrap-up); stay 6, 12 or 18 months. All-inclusive coaching (business development, go-to-market and sales, recruitment, product and IP, impact, marketing and PR, partner perks, financing and investor relations), office space at A House in Stockholm, and a pitch at the invite-only Sting Demo Day with 200+ handpicked investors.
- **Amount:** Possible 600 000 SEK (400 000 + 200 000) via Propel Capital, usually as a standardized convertible debt instrument. Success fee: up to 2% Sting warrant per 6-month block (excluding the Propel investment).
- **Requirements:** Swedish company; MVP/prototype stage; climate, health, AI or deep tech; two co-founders; 75% founder ownership.
- **Before you apply:** 1. Pitch deck of 10-15 slides uploaded in the HubSpot form (mandatory), covering traction, team, cap table and a 12-18 month roadmap; the form also asks for founders, cap table and prior accelerator programs. 2. Mandatory consent that Sting may use AI tools to evaluate the application; keep sensitive IP out of the submission. 3. Optional 3-minute video pitch URL, 'not required, but strongly encouraged'. 4. Pitch to the Sting selection committee at a selection final after written screening. 5. Physical presence in Stockholm during key parts of the program (desk at A House). 6. Success fee: up to 2% Sting warrant per 6-month block, converted into shares after the program. 7. Optional Propel Capital convertible of 400 000 + 200 000 SEK: the 400K converts at a 30% discount with a 20 MSEK cap, the 200K at round terms; full terms only on request. 8. Apply by the cohort deadline (generally mid-August and late January); late applications are tagged 'late' and compete for fewer slots.
- **Hard gates:** Team: 'At least two committed co-founders working 50–100%, with 75% or more of the company owned by the operational team'. Tech readiness: 'MVP/prototype stage (TRL 3–6)'.
- **Unclear:** Team rule conflicts: the Core page asks for 'at least two committed co-founders', the FAQ says solo founders can apply; ask hello@sting.co whether a solo founder is screened out. The FAQ says applicants are 'registered in Sweden as AB companies', but the form's Swedish org-number field is optional; ask whether the AB is needed at application or only at program start. Cohort timing is given three ways (blocks 'Oct-Mar, Mar-Sept'; '2 cohorts/year: April & October'; FAQ 'October and March'); the page says the next program kicks off in early 2027 but prints no 2027 deadline, so ask whether the late-January deadline applies. Cohort size is '12-16' in one block and '16–20 startups/cohort' in another. Propel figure inconsistent: 600 000 SEK on the Core page and in the Propel body text, but 'up to 500,000 SEK' in the site's Science to Business teaser and '500 000 SEK' in the Propel page header. The Propel investment is 'optional and offered to selected companies'; the selection criteria are not published. Warrant terms (strike price, what decides 'up to 2%') are not published. Program language not stated; the site and form are in English.
- **How to apply:** HubSpot application form behind "Apply for Sting Core": https://share.hsforms.com/2gw10xJqxRF--BnigBjZ_HQ50drh. Also https://app.sting.co/auth/signup.
- **Status page:** https://www.sting.co/sting-core
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Investment: Possible 600,000 SEK (400 + 200K) via Propel Capital Success Fee: Up to 2% Sting Warrant per 6 month block (excluding Propel Capital investment)"
- **Gotchas:** "Our next program kicks off in early 2027"; no deadline stated and the page does not say whether applications are open. Two cohorts per year (blocks Oct-Mar and Mar-Sept), 12-16 startups per cohort. sting.co is inconsistent on the Propel amount (500 000 vs 600 000 SEK) and the success fee wording ("up to 2%" vs "2% option / 6 months").

Attend all four in-person evening sessions, 17:00-20:30 every two weeks, at A House Ark plus Kista Science City (DeepTech) or Karolinska (Health). 3. Homework before each workshop; coming prepared is called essential. 4. Sign a confidentiality agreement at the first workshop. 5. Applications are reviewed only after the deadline; answer within two weeks (spring 2026: notification by April 9). 6. Choose one track: DeepTech, Health & Life Science, or Climate & Energy.

Not stated whether teams must be based in the Stockholm region; the DeepTech page calls it a 'national pre-accelerator program' but all sessions are in Stockholm. Capacity is '10–15 teams' on the track pages and 'Between 12-15 projects' in the FAQ. Workshop language not stated; the pages and form are in English. Not stated whether a company is needed; the form asks for 'Your project/company'.
- **How to apply:** Track pages under https://www.sting.co/pre-sting/test-drive (DeepTech, Health & Life Science, Climate & Energy); no live form on 2026-10-15.
