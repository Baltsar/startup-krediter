# Cloud credits

Vendor credit programs for infrastructure and developer tooling. The credits sit with the vendor, so the founder's stack decides which door exists at all: an AWS startup cannot spend Google's credits. Every program below has a self-serve floor that a bootstrapped founder can claim alone (AWS Founders 1 000-5 000 USD, Google Start 2 000 USD, Microsoft 1 000 USD then 4 000 USD, Cloudflare Tier 3 10 000 USD, Neon 1 000 USD), but the headline amounts are partner-locked: AWS Portfolio needs an Activate Provider Org ID, Google Scale needs institutional equity funding, Microsoft's investor offer needs an Investor Network referral code, Cloudflare Tier 1 and 2 need funding from an affiliated partner, Vercel is only open to companies backed by a listed Startup Partner, and Neon's venture-backed tier needs 1 MUSD raised or a recognized accelerator. The practical first question is therefore "which VC or accelerator is on your cap table, and which vendor programs are they enrolled in". Credits expire (typically 12 months, Google and Microsoft 2 years) and most programs are first-time only, so timing the application to when spend actually starts matters.

Not covered here: AI API credits (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google AI Studio and similar sit in the ai-api-credits domain), perks marketplaces and accelerator-bundled deals, reseller or regional cloud programs, and compute grants on public HPC systems. All pages below were read on 2026-10-15; tier names and amounts are copied as written that day and change often, so re-read the status page before quoting a number.

## AWS Activate Credits {#aws-activate}

- **Who:** Startups from self-funded to pre-Series B, founded in the last 10 years, with an AWS account on a paid tier plan, and either new to Activate Credits or requesting more than previously received.
- **What:** Promotional AWS credits in two self-applied tiers (Activate Founders for self-funded startups, Activate Portfolio for startups with an Organization ID from an Activate Provider) plus an invite-only AI tier above them.
- **Amount:** Activate Founders: up to 5 000 USD, starting at 1 000 USD ("Select participants may qualify for additional credits up to $5,000"). Activate Portfolio: up to 200 000 USD. AWS Credits for AI Startups: 200 000 USD+, invite only, "Talk with your Account Manager".
- **Requirements:** Pre-Series B; founded in the last 10 years; AWS Account on Paid Tier Plan; new to Activate Credits or asking for more than before. Portfolio additionally requires an Org ID from an Activate Provider such as an accelerator, angel investor or VC firm (provider list: https://aws.amazon.com/startups/providers). Builder ID is created with a professional email.
- **Before you apply:** 1. AWS Builder ID verified by email and an Activate profile with a business email on your own domain. 2. AWS account on a paid account plan (free plans are not eligible), linked to the Builder ID with root or admin access. 3. A fully functioning company website; AWS evaluates public information from it. 4. Portfolio tier: an Org ID or email invitation from your Activate Provider (an investor or accelerator in AWS's program). 5. Founders tier: any startup without a provider Org ID can apply, funded or not, up to and including Series A. 6. Your latest funding round, if you have had one, within the last 12 months; AWS lists an older round among its rejection reasons. 7. Product, target market, traction, funding stage and date of the latest round in the form. 8. Identity verification with an ID document when AWS asks; declining means rejection. 9. Portfolio tier: the provider must confirm your association by answering AWS Activate's verification email, or the application is declined. 10. Credits also pay for third-party models on Amazon Bedrock, Anthropic's Claude included; to use them for Claude you call it through Bedrock, not Anthropic's own API.
- **Unclear:** Review time differs across pages: 5 to 10 or 7 to 10 business days. Founders tier: 1 000 USD stated, 'select startups may qualify for up to 5 000 USD' with no criteria; Portfolio amounts depend on the provider and are not published. The FAQ answer on why applications are declined is a broken placeholder.
- **How to apply:** Create or sign in to an AWS Builder ID at https://aws.amazon.com/startups/join?destination=/credits/apply (redirects to startups.aws.com), choose Founders or Portfolio, describe product, funding stage and company, link an AWS account, and the founder clicks submit. Reply within 5-10 business days.
- **Status page:** https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits/
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Eligibility for AWS Activate: Pre-series B, founded in the last 10 years, AWS Account on Paid Tier Plan, and either new to Activate Credits or requesting more credits than previously received"
- **Gotchas:** The 200 000 USD figure is Portfolio only and partner-locked; without a provider Org ID the ceiling is 5 000 USD and the default grant is 1 000 USD. The page does not state a credit expiry; that lives in the AWS Promotional Credit Terms (https://aws.amazon.com/activate/terms/). Kiro Startup Credits are a separate offer for startups without current Activate Credits.

## Google for Startups Cloud Program {#google-cloud-startup}

- **Who:** Digital-native technology startups from pre-funded to Series A. Start tier: working MVP, clear business model, plans to seek venture funding soon, founded within the last 24 months. Scale tier: recent equity funding (pre-seed to Series A) from an institutional investor or common Web3 funding sources, founded within the last 5 years.
- **What:** Google Cloud credits over two years, covering Google Cloud services including Gemini and Gemma models (not third-party or Marketplace services), with support, Workspace and training extras. Series B+ startups get custom solutions instead of program credits.
- **Amount:** Start tier: 2 000 USD. Scale tier: up to 200 000 USD over 2 years, structured as "Year 1: 100% up to $100,000 USD in Google Cloud credits" and "Year 2: 20% up to an additional $100,000 USD in Google Cloud credits". AI-first Scale: up to 350 000 USD ("For AI-first startups: 100% up to $250,000 USD in Google Cloud credits" in year 1, same year 2). Scale also lists 12 000 USD Enhanced Support credits for one year, 12 months of Google Workspace Business Plus, 600 USD per month of Google Maps credit for one year and 500 USD Google Skills credits.
- **Requirements:** Business email matching the startup's public website domain; 18-character Google Cloud billing account ID. Start: no Google Cloud credits beyond the free trial. Scale: not more than 5 000 USD in prior Google Cloud credits; funding verification if in stealth. AI-first: uses or plans to use Gemini as the foundation of the primary product; pre-seed/seed within the last five years, Series A within the last 12 months. Private equity, government innovation grants, prize funding, crowdfunding, angel and friends-and-family money do not qualify for Scale but may be evaluated for Start. FAQ exclusions: IPO'd or acquired companies, educational institutions, government entities, nonprofits, personal blogs or content, dev shops.
- **Before you apply:** 1. Google account sign-in; the apply page redirects to accounts.google.com. 2. Google Cloud account with its 18-character billing account ID. 3. Business email on the same domain as your public website and the billing account. 4. Public proof of equity funding (news, Crunchbase, investor portfolio) for the Scale tier; stealth startups verify through their investor. 5. Decision in a few business days; manual review can take 10 days or more.
- **Unclear:** Scale headline differs: up to 350 000 USD on one page, 200 000 plus 150 000 USD for 'AI-first qualified' startups on another, at Google's discretion. Start tier asks for 'plans to seek venture funding soon' and a 'digital-native' startup; neither is defined. Expiry of Scale credits is set at issuance and only shown in the console.
- **How to apply:** Create a Google Cloud account to get the billing account ID, open https://cloud.google.com/startup/apply (Google sign-in required), apply with the matching business email, and the founder clicks submit. Most decisions within a few business days; manual review 10 days or more.
- **Status page:** https://cloud.google.com/startup (tier pages: https://cloud.google.com/startup/pre-funded, https://cloud.google.com/startup/early-stage, https://cloud.google.com/startup/ai, FAQ https://cloud.google.com/startup/faq)
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Build and grow with $200,000 in cloud credits (or up to $350,000 for AI-first startups) through the Google for Startups Cloud Program."
- **Gotchas:** The year 2 credit is 20% of actual spend, so the 200 000 / 350 000 USD ceilings assume 500 000 USD of year-2 spend. Angel-only rounds do not count as equity funding for Scale. The main /startup page carries almost no eligibility text; it lives on the tier pages and the FAQ. The apply flow sits behind a Google login so it could not be read automatically.

## Microsoft for Startups {#microsoft-for-startups}

- **Who:** Privately held, for-profit startups that own a software product, headquartered where Azure is available, not Series C or later, with under 350 000 USD in lifetime free Azure credits. Not educational institutions, government organizations, consultancies, agencies or crypto mining.
- **What:** Azure Startup credits that unlock progressively, plus AI tools, technical guidance and go-to-market resources. Restructured in 2025-2026: since 2026-05-29 new applicants apply and activate through the Azure portal rather than the old Founders Hub.
- **Amount:** Marketing page: "up to $150,000 in credits". Learn overview: "Unlock up to $150K in Startup credits over time as your startup demonstrates verified progress, service adoption, and sustained Azure usage." Without an investor code: "$1,000 for 90 days, then an additional $4,000 for 180 days after business verification" (Learn FAQ). Investor offer: "most startups begin with $100,000, with the opportunity to earn more" (Learn FAQ). No tier names are published on any page read today.
- **Requirements:** The eligibility list above (Learn overview, updated 2026-08-05). The investor offer requires a 10-character referral code from a Microsoft for Startups Investor Network partner (investor, accelerator, incubator, angel network, university); Microsoft does not publish the partner list. A credit card is needed to activate; credits must be activated within 90 days of accepting the agreement and are valid up to two years with no extensions.
- **Before you apply:** 1. A personal Microsoft account (Gmail, Outlook or similar) with no prior Azure account; work-domain emails are rejected at sign-up. 2. Registered startup name and address exactly as on legal documents, industry and website; mismatches fail business verification. 3. Credit card for the Azure account and a human check; the country cannot be changed later. 4. Personal LinkedIn sign-in for identity on the no-code path; a 10-character investor referral code for the investor offer. 5. Business verification (proof of a registered legal entity) to move past the starter credits.
- **Unclear:** Starter credit differs by page: 200 USD or 1 000 USD, then 4 000 USD more after business verification. Stage limit differs: 'not Series C or later' or 'pre-seed through Series B, B2B'. What business verification requires is not stated beyond matching name and address. The partner list for referral codes is not published.
- **How to apply:** Open https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/startups, click Get started now (leads to portal.azure.com with the startup home view), sign in with a Microsoft account, enter the referral code or continue without one, verify email and phone, enter registered company details, set up the Azure account with a payment card, and the founder clicks submit. Decision by email in about three business days.
- **Status page:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/startups/microsoft-for-startups/overview (amount also on https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/startups; FAQ https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/startups/microsoft-for-startups/mfs-faqs; activation guide https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/startups/build/azure-getting-started/activate)
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Unlock up to $150K in Startup credits over time as your startup demonstrates verified progress, service adoption, and sustained Azure usage."
- **Gotchas:** The 150 000 USD is a ceiling reached over time, not a grant on approval; without an investor code the realistic first-year figure is 5 000 USD. "Not Series C or later" cannot be expressed with the stage enum, so the matcher does not filter on it. Credits are tied to the Microsoft account used at registration and cannot be moved; work or school (Entra ID) accounts cause activation errors.

## Cloudflare for Startups {#cloudflare-for-startups}

- **Who:** For-profit technology startups incorporated within the last 10 years, funded up to Series B and funded within the last 12 months (self-funded accepted at Tier 3), with a live public website, a public presence on LinkedIn, X or GitHub, and a business email matching the domain. First-time applicants only.
- **What:** One year of Cloudflare credits across the developer platform (Workers, Workers for Platforms, Durable Objects, Workflows, Workers AI, KV, D1, Queues, Vectorize, R2, Pages, Images, Stream, Cache Reserve, Argo) with core security and networking included at no cost.
- **Amount:** Tier 3: 10 000 USD, "Bootstrapped or self-funded", "Under $1M raised". Tier 2: 100 000 USD, "Under $5M raised", "Funded by an affiliated partner". Tier 1: 350 000 USD, "$5M+ raised", "Funded by an affiliated partner".
- **Requirements:** All of: founded within the last 10 years; funded up to Series B; funded within the last 12 months; actively developing a technology product; valid publicly accessible website; active on LinkedIn, X or GitHub; not previously approved; for-profit with a business email matching the domain. Caps: R2 10 000 USD; Workers AI 2 500 / 10 000 / 50 000 USD by tier. Excluded: AI Gateway (temporarily), registrar, enterprise add-ons, Magic Transit, Enterprise Spectrum, Dedicated IPs, China Network. Not eligible: educational institutions, personal blogs, consultancies, agencies, MSPs, resellers, existing enterprise customers.
- **Before you apply:** 1. The Apply button opens an Airtable form behind an Airtable sign-in. 2. Business email on your own domain; personal addresses are rejected. 3. A live public website and a public LinkedIn, X or GitHub presence. 4. Funding raised within the last 12 months and the partner's name for Tier 2 or Tier 1. 5. Cloudflare account with a valid payment method; overages are billed to it. 6. Consent to Cloudflare using your company name and logo in marketing.
- **Unclear:** 'Affiliated partner' for the higher tiers is not defined and no partner list is published; ask startups@cloudflare.com. 'Funded within the last 12 months' is listed for everyone, including bootstrapped Tier 3 startups. The form fields could not be read without an Airtable login. The 'Apply here' link at the bottom of the page returns 404.
- **How to apply:** Form at https://www.cloudflare.com/lp/startups; the founder clicks submit. Review typically up to 48 hours, confirmation by email. Questions to startups@cloudflare.com.
- **Status page:** https://www.cloudflare.com/startups/ (https://www.cloudflare.com/forstartups/ redirects here)
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15 (program page). The application page https://www.cloudflare.com/lp/startups returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches and was not read.
- **Evidence:** "Cloudflare for Startups gives early-stage companies up to $350k in credits to build the next big idea."
- **Gotchas:** "Funded within the last 12 months" is listed as required for everyone, including bootstrapped applicants; how Cloudflare reads that for self-funded companies is unclear. Credits run one year from the confirmation email and are never extended. Tiers were raised recently (Tier 3 from 5 000 to 10 000 USD, Tier 1 from 250 000 to 350 000 USD); members under old caps can request an upgrade evaluation.

## Vercel for Startups {#vercel-for-startups}

- **Who:** Startups affiliated with an approved Vercel Startup Partner (accelerators and VC firms on Vercel's dropdown list), Series A or less, applying within 12 months of their most recent round, with a company website and a matching email, and no prior Vercel for Startups credits.
- **What:** An enterprise-grade contract with a Flexible Commitment Amount usable across seats, data transfer, compute, AI Gateway, v0, Eve, Workflows and Sandboxes, plus Enterprise-tier access for a year, Slack access to the Vercel team, events and swag.
- **Amount:** Up to 30 000 USD Flexible Commitment Amount; "The Flexible Commitment Amount depends on the arrangement between Vercel and your Startup Partner and may vary between partners, up to a maximum of $30,000."
- **Requirements:** Partner affiliation with proof (screenshot of partner dashboard, hub, wiki or console); Series A or less; within 12 months of the latest round; company website and matching email; not previously funded by the program; valid payment method on file. At most 50% of the commitment may go to AI Gateway fees.
- **Before you apply:** 1. Vercel account and team created before applying; the contract binds to that team. 2. Email on the same domain as the company website. 3. Your accelerator or VC must be in the Startup Partner dropdown; otherwise the VC files a partner request first. 4. Screenshot of the whole browser window showing the Vercel offer in the partner's dashboard or hub; acceptance emails are not accepted. 5. Apply within 12 months of your latest round; no previous Vercel for Startups credits. 6. Valid payment method on the team before credits activate.
- **Unclear:** The amount depends on the deal between Vercel and your partner and is not published; only the 30 000 USD ceiling is stated. The form says 'Free Vercel Pro' while the FAQ describes an Enterprise-terms contract. Credits are 'at Vercel's sole discretion' and can be revoked at any time.
- **How to apply:** Open https://vercel.com/startups/credits, enter email, select or create the Vercel team, pick the Startup Partner, upload proof, and the founder clicks submit. Review 5-7 days; on approval accept the Flexible Commitment for Startups Terms, after which credits and Enterprise access go live within 5-7 business days.
- **Status page:** https://vercel.com/startups/credits (the marketing page https://vercel.com/startups has only an interest form; https://vercel.com/startupsprogram is a 404)
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "The Vercel for Startups program is an exclusive offer to companies backed by our Startup Partners."
- **Gotchas:** No self-serve tier at all; founders without a listed partner must get their VC to file the partner form at https://vercel.com/startups/partners. The contract binds one team and cannot be transferred. Unused commitment does not roll over at the one-year mark, and once spent the team drops to standard list rates. Terms described here apply to startups accepted on or after 2026-08-06.

## Supabase {#supabase-startups}

- **Who:** No public startup credit program was found on supabase.com on 2026-10-15.
- **What:** https://supabase.com/startups is a marketing page ("Supabase is for Startups") with a generic sign-up button and no credits, tiers or application. https://supabase.com/partners is a technology and agency partner program, not a founder program. The docs page https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/credits describes billing credits, not a startup offer. Probed and 404: /startup-program, /partners/startups, /startups/apply, /credits.
- **Amount:** None published by Supabase. Third-party aggregators describe partner-channel perks (for example a Supabase offer listed in AWS Activate offers, and VC or accelerator perk bundles); these were not verified against a Supabase page and are not recorded.
- **Requirements:** Not applicable.
- **How to apply:** No direct door. Founders in an accelerator or on AWS Activate should look in their partner's perk list for a Supabase offer.
- **Status page:** https://supabase.com/startups
- **Verified:** Not verified on 2026-10-15: no public program page exists on supabase.com; the AWS Activate offer page https://aws.amazon.com/startups/offers/supabase redirects to an AWS sign-in and could not be read.
- **Evidence:** "Speed wins. Supabase helps you move faster by providing a full-featured, scalable backend based on Postgres."
- **Gotchas:** No JSON record. Aggregator claims (six months of the Team plan, 3 000 USD via partners) conflict with each other and have no primary source.

## Neon Credits for Startups (Databricks Startup Program) {#neon-startups}

- **Who:** Early-stage startups building a product or MVP on Neon. Self-Funded tier: "Early-stage, self-funded startups. Less than $1M in funding." Venture-Backed tier: "VC-backed startups with at least $1M in funding. Participants in recognized accelerators (e.g. Y Combinator)."
- **What:** Neon credits (Self-Funded) or Neon and Databricks credits (Venture-Backed), onboarding support from the Neon product team, early access to new features; the venture tier adds speaking and co-marketing opportunities.
- **Amount:** Self-Funded: "Up to $1,000 in Neon credits." Venture-Backed: "Up to $200K in Neon and Databricks credits." "The exact amount you receive depends on your stage and funding."
- **Requirements:** Tier rules above; building an early-stage product or MVP. Applicants agree to Databricks' Terms of Use and Privacy Notice. Existing Neon or Databricks users can apply.
- **Before you apply:** 1. Neon account on a paid plan with a payment method on file, plus the Neon Org ID. 2. Databricks account with a credit card and its Account ID if you also claim Databricks credits. 3. Company email on your own domain, website, country of incorporation, year founded, launch date. 4. One proof of funding: accelerator verification link, a partner code from your VC, a VC contact, or a public link. 5. LinkedIn sign-in before you can submit. 6. Mission, technical challenges and how you use data or AI in at most 250 characters.
- **Unclear:** The form says venture-backed only (at least 1 MUSD or a recognised accelerator) and shows 100 000 USD per product, while neon.com/startups lists a Self-Funded tier of up to 1 000 USD that the form does not offer. The paid-plan and credit-card requirement is only on the form. The exact amount 'depends on your stage and funding'; no table is published.
- **How to apply:** Form at https://sites.google.com/databricks.com/startup-program-apply (Google Sites, reachable); the founder clicks submit. Reply within a few business days; approved credits are added to the existing account.
- **Status page:** https://neon.com/startups
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Qualifying startups can receive up to $200K in credits for Neon and Databricks."
- **Gotchas:** The page is branded "Databricks Startup Program" (Neon is part of Databricks), and the 200 000 USD ceiling is split across Neon and Databricks, so a Neon-only startup will not see the whole amount as Neon credits. Credits are valid 12 months from acceptance and then billing continues on the chosen plan. No company-age or website rule is stated.

## MongoDB for Startups {#mongodb-for-startups}

- **Who:** Startups less than 7 years old at Series A or earlier, focused on a single software-based product or service (development shops and agencies not eligible), with an active company website and LinkedIn profile, not previously accepted.
- **What:** MongoDB Atlas credits via promo code, Voyage AI tokens, technical expertise and support, in four tiers: Inspire (bootstrapped), Grow (early-funded), Innovate (rising VC-backed), Scale (rapidly scaling).
- **Amount:** Not published. The page describes tiers only as "baseline credits", "expanded credits", "additional credits" and "maximum credits"; the only dollar figure is that Atlas Developer Support starts at 49 USD per month after a free 30 days.
- **Requirements:** The base-tier list above. Partner referral is optional: "Please ask your VC or accelerator partner to refer you", otherwise apply directly on the page. Activation needs the Organization Owner or Organization Billing Admin role. The form requires company email, legal name, company URL, LinkedIn (personal accepted if the company has none), HQ country and a funding stage (Bootstrapped, Pre-seed, Seed, Series A, Series B or higher).
- **Before you apply:** 1. Company email as the applicant email. 2. Company URL and a LinkedIn profile to confirm affiliation. 3. Legal name, HQ country, postal code and funding stage in the form. 4. Referral from your VC or accelerator if they are a partner; otherwise apply directly. 5. A MongoDB Atlas organisation where an Organization Owner or Billing Admin applies the code within 12 months.
- **Unclear:** Credit amounts per tier (Inspire, Grow, Innovate, Scale) and tier placement are not published. The linked terms are 2018 sweepstakes rules that do not match the program; confirm with startups@mongodb.com. Whether a partner referral gives a higher tier than applying directly is not stated.
- **How to apply:** Apply Now form at https://www.mongodb.com/solutions/startups#startups; the founder clicks submit. Chase startups@mongodb.com after five business days of silence.
- **Status page:** https://www.mongodb.com/solutions/startups (https://www.mongodb.com/startups redirects here)
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "At a minimum, to qualify for our base tier, we look for startups that are: Early-stage: Less than 7 years old and Series A or earlier"
- **Gotchas:** No amount can be quoted; the record carries text only. Twelve months to activate the promo code, then twelve months to spend it; extensions are case by case and should be requested about three months before expiry. Promo codes never apply to past charges.
