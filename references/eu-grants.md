# EU grants

EU-level grant doors that a Nordic startup can walk through without a national intermediary, all funded under Horizon Europe. Sweden, Denmark and Finland are EU members; Norway and Iceland are associated to Horizon Europe, so the same doors are open to them. Two programs carry full records: the EIC Accelerator (single-company grant plus equity, TRL 6-8) and Eurostars (SME-led international R&D consortia, paid by national agencies). Reference-only lines cover the research-stage EIC Pathfinder, Horizon Europe cascade funding (FSTP) and the Enterprise Europe Network as the free advisory door. Not covered here: national programs (see nordic-grants), compute allocations such as EuroHPC (see compute), EIB or national loans, Horizon Europe collaborative calls that require a research consortium (RIA/IA), ERC, Marie Skłodowska-Curie, LIFE, Digital Europe, Interreg and ESA BIC. All facts below come from pages read on 2026-10-15; anything dated must be re-read on the status page before it is quoted.

## EIC Accelerator {#eic-accelerator}

- **Who:** A single start-up or SME (including spin-outs) from an EU Member State or a Horizon Europe associated country, or a natural person or legal entity about to set up an SME. Small mid-caps with up to 499 employees may apply for investment only. UK applicants: grant only. Third-country applicants must relocate before the full application.
- **What:** Blended finance for innovation at TRL 6-8: a lump-sum grant component below EUR 2.5 million to be completed within 24 months, plus an investment component of EUR 1-10 million as direct equity or quasi-equity (convertible loans). Grant only and investment only are also possible. All supported companies get Business Acceleration Services (coaches, mentors, investors, partners).
- **Amount:** Grant component below EUR 2.5 million; investment component EUR 1-10 million (higher amounts via STEP Scale-Up). Budgets 2026: Accelerator Open EUR 414 million, Accelerator Challenges EUR 220 million.
- **Requirements:** Single company (no consortium). SME, or small mid-cap for investment only. Based in an EU Member State or Horizon Europe associated country. Only one application at a time; a new one only after the results of the previous. Grant only is awarded once per company during Horizon Europe 2021-2027.
- **Before you apply:** 1. EU Login and a PIC number on the EU Funding and Tenders portal. 2. Step 1 short application: a 12-page form, a pitch deck of at most 10 slides as PDF, and a video pitch of at most 3 minutes by up to three core team members. 3. Step 2 full application on the portal: a 20-page form, pitch deck, implementation plan, financial information, letters of intent, a freedom-to-operate analysis and a 3-minute video. 4. Submit Step 2 before a batching date at 17:00 Brussels time; the 2026 page lists 4 November 2026 as the last. 5. Step 3: an interview with the EIC Jury if your proposal ranks high enough. 6. Only one application at a time; a new one only after the previous result. A third-country applicant must relocate before Step 2.
- **Unclear:** The topic page on the Funding and Tenders portal refuses automated reads; check the 2027 cut-off dates there in a browser. The 2026 batching dates are written in mixed formats on the page (7/01/2026, 04/03/2026); confirm the next date before planning.
- **How to apply:** Four steps on the EU Funding and Tenders portal (topic HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01). Step 1, short application: 12-page form, pitch deck of up to ten slides, three-minute video; may be submitted at any time, batched every first Tuesday of the month at 17:00 Brussels time, feedback in about 4-6 weeks. Step 2, full application after a GO: 20-page form, pitch deck, implementation plan, financial information, letters of intent, FTO analysis, three-minute video; batched at 17:00 Brussels time on the 2026 dates written on the page as 7/01/2026, 04/03/2026, 06/05/2026, 08/07/2026, 02/09/2026 and 04/11/2026 (day/month/year; the only one still ahead on 2026-10-15 is 4 November 2026); remote result within 8-9 weeks. Step 3, interview with the EIC Jury, organised after each second batch; results 2-3 weeks after the interview week. Step 4, grant agreement signature and due diligence for the investment component (decided within 2-6 months). The founder clicks submit on the portal.
- **Status page:** https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Short proposals may be submitted at any time and will be batched for evaluation every first Tuesday of the month at 5pm Brussels time."
- **Gotchas:** The page gives no open/closed status; the apply link goes to the portal topic for 2026, and no 2027 cut-off dates are published on the page yet. Top-ranked full proposals corresponding to 2.5 times the available grant budget are invited to interview; proposals that pass the 13/15 threshold but are not funded get a Seal of Excellence (plus a STEP seal for Challenges). Fast Track (existing Horizon Europe or Horizon 2020 projects) and Plug-In (certified national programmes) skip straight to the full application. The page says the EIC "particularly welcomes applications from startups and SMEs with female CEOs" (a preference, not a rule). The Work Programme 2026 PDF on the page is dated 17 June 2026 and the full-proposal annexes 9 July 2026. National Contact Points (linked from the page) give free guidance.

## Eurostars {#eurostars}

- **Who:** A consortium of at least two entities that are independent of one another, from at least two Eurostars countries (at least one from an EU or Horizon Europe associated country), led by an innovative SME from a Eurostars country. Other partners can be any type of organisation. Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland and Iceland are all listed as participating in Call 12.
- **What:** A grant for an international collaborative R&D project of 36 months or less with an exclusive focus on civil applications. Eureka evaluates centrally; funding is decentralised and each partner is paid by its own national funding body under national rules (Vinnova in Sweden, the Research Council of Norway, Business Finland, Innovation Fund Denmark, Rannís in Iceland).
- **Amount:** No central amount. Per the Call 12 page: Sweden, Vinnova funds SMEs up to 50% of project costs, maximum 5 million SEK per project (large companies, universities and research organisations: 2 MSEK, or 5 MSEK if a Swedish SME is in the consortium). Norway, Research Council of Norway up to 50% of project costs for SMEs. Finland, Business Finland 50% of eligible costs for growth-oriented SMEs with no stated upper limit. Denmark, Innovation Fund Denmark maximum 300 000 EUR per participant (500 000 EUR in total for several Danish participants, minimum 50 000 EUR). Iceland, Rannís maximum 45 million ISK per project. National details were marked as still being confirmed and subject to change before the call opens.
- **Requirements:** The seven international criteria: SME lead from a Eurostars country; at least two independent entities; at least two Eurostars countries with at least one EU or Horizon Europe associated country; SMEs from Eurostars countries carry 50% or more of the total project cost excluding subcontracting; no single participant or country above 70% of the budget; duration 36 months or less; civil applications only. National criteria apply on top (Vinnova checks the latest registered annual report and credit reports).
- **Before you apply:** 1. A consortium of at least two independent organisations from at least two Eurostars countries, led by an SME; SMEs carry at least half the budget and no participant or country more than 70 percent. 2. Every partner checks eligibility with its own national funding body before applying; Vinnova for a Swedish partner. 3. Application on the Eureka platform myeurekaproject.org, Call 12 open 17 December 2026 to 4 March 2027 at 14:00 Brussels time. 4. A project of at most 36 months with an exclusively civil application.
- **Unclear:** No call is open on 2026-10-16; national rules for Call 12 are marked as being confirmed and may change before it opens. Each national funding body pays its own partner under national rules and may cap the amount; Eureka publishes no central amount.
- **How to apply:** Build the consortium, read the national rules for every partner, then submit the international application on the Eureka project platform (myeurekaproject.org) while a call is open. Call 12: opens 17 December 2026, deadline 4 March 2027 14:00 Brussels time. Swedish participants file a national application to Vinnova after a positive evaluation; Finnish participants file a parallel application with Business Finland at submission; Danish participants upload the application to e-grant within 14 days after the deadline. The founder, as lead applicant, clicks submit on the platform.
- **Status page:** https://www.eurekanetwork.org/programmes/eurostars (lists the current call; national amounts live on the call page https://www.eurekanetwork.org/programmes-and-calls/eurostars/eurostars-call-12-for-projects-deadline-march-2027/)
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "You can submit your R&D and innovation project application between 17 December 2026 and 4 March 2027 14:00 (Brussels time) using our project platform (myeurekaproject.org)."
- **Gotchas:** No Eurostars call is open on 2026-10-15; the Eureka open-calls listing shows none, and Call 12 does not open until 17 December 2026. Calls are usually organised twice a year. The programme page's statistics render as 0 without JavaScript. The Norwegian funder is the Research Council of Norway, not Innovasjon Norge. Greece, Israel, Italy and Brussels have no budget for Call 12, so partners from there would have to self-fund. Exchange rates for SEK and ISK caps are fixed on the day the call opens (Sweden) or the deadline (Iceland).

## Reference-only lines

These are doors worth knowing about but not records in the matcher: they are either research-stage, project-specific, or advisory rather than funding.

### EIC Pathfinder {#eic-pathfinder}

Research-stage deep tech (TRL 1-4), "funding research to develop the scientific basis to underpin breakthrough technologies". Who: "Consortia of at least 3 different independent legal entities established in different countries or single applicants (Pathfinder Challenges only)" from EU Member States and Horizon Europe associated countries; the page addresses "Research teams", so it is academia-heavy. Grants of up to EUR 4 million (or more if duly justified) plus booster grants not exceeding EUR 50 000. Budgets 2026: Pathfinder Open EUR 166 million, Pathfinder Challenges EUR 96 million. Deadlines on the page: Pathfinder Open 12 May 2026 (passed), Pathfinder Challenges 28 October 2026. Page: https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-pathfinder_en . Verified 2026-10-15. A startup fits only as a partner in a research consortium or, for a Challenge, as a single applicant with research capacity.

### Horizon Europe cascade funding (FSTP) {#horizon-cascade-funding}

Many Horizon Europe projects re-grant part of their budget to third parties, typically SMEs and startups, through their own open calls ("financial support to third parties"); amounts and rules are set per project. The EU Funding and Tenders portal lists them under competitive calls: https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/competitive-calls-cs . Not verified on 2026-10-15: the portal page is rendered by JavaScript and returns no content to an automated fetch, so no amounts or dates are recorded here. Check the portal in a browser or ask the Enterprise Europe Network.

### Enterprise Europe Network {#enterprise-europe-network}

The free advisory door for finding EU calls: "We identify EU funding opportunities that suit your business' needs and help you apply", alongside "Access to finance" ("We help you identify sources of finance and ensure your business is investor ready"). Local contact points exist in every EU country and in Norway and Iceland; start at https://een.ec.europa.eu/ and use "Find a local Network contact point". Verified 2026-10-15 (the site returned 403 to WebFetch but 200 to curl). Run by EISMEA, the same agency that runs the EIC.

## EUDIS Business Accelerator (EU defence accelerator, Cohort 4, spring 2027) {#eudis-business-accelerator}

- **Who:** Start-ups and scale-ups registered in an EU Member State or Norway, with executive management there and not controlled from outside the EU or Norway, aiming to enter or grow in the European defence market with a defence or dual-use product that fits a programme technology theme. Hard Yes/No checks: registered legal entity in the EU or Norway; theme relevance; at least EUR 200 000 raised (dilutive or non-dilutive) or yearly revenue over EUR 200 000; an established management team with full-time staff covering technology and business development; company at most 20 years old. A functioning prototype or product with initial traction is also required. Iceland is not eligible.
- **What:** An 8-month defence accelerator run for the European Commission (DG DEFIS) by Starburst, Civitta, Ignity and VTT: over 300 hours of expert coaching per company, five on-site bootcamps of 2-3 days across Europe, guidance to test facilities, and meetings with defence end-users, industry and investors. 20 companies per cohort; two cohorts in 2027 (spring from March, fall from September). No equity or IP is taken.
- **Amount:** "Non-dilutive seed voucher of 120 000€" per company, paid 20/80 against a scorecard and only after the full programme. An extra reward voucher goes to top performers; no amount is given for Cohort 4.
- **Requirements:** Company registered in an EU Member State or Norway; not controlled from a non-associated third country (or holding an approved Article 9(4) EDF guarantee); at most 20 years old; EUR 200 000 raised or yearly revenue over EUR 200 000; product with traction; defence market focus within a technology theme.
- **Before you apply:** 1. Application form linked from eudis-business-accelerator.eu, before 16 November 2026 at 23:59 CET; only a company's last submission counts. 2. Ownership-and-control declaration on the Commission's template, uploaded in the form. 3. Evaluation deck of at most 15 slides (technology, value proposition, go-to-market, 2025 sales and 2026 target, team, mission readiness); a longer deck is not considered. 4. Financial capability: EUR 200 000 raised or yearly revenue over EUR 200 000. 5. Management team and full-time staff covering technology and business development. 6. A prototype, product or service with early revenue, pilots or pre-commercial validation, and a documented business plan. 7. One of three themes: autonomous systems and robotics; protection against drones and low-cost threats; defence production, sustainment and logistics (an open disruptive-technology topic only if none fits). 8. If among the top 40 (by 30 November 2026): a 7-minute pitch plus 5 minutes of questions, 14-16 December 2026, to a board with the Commission and the European Defence Agency. 9. If selected: 8 months from March 2027 with five bootcamps of mandatory attendance; resources used must be in the EU or Norway. 10. The voucher is paid only on completion, 20 and 80 percent, unlocked by the scorecard agreed at kick-off.
- **Unclear:** The guidelines ask for EUR 200 000 in total funding; the form asks about money raised "in the past year". The Cohort 4 top-performer reward has no amount (the Commission page gave EUR 50 000 for the top 3 in 2026). Whether the voucher is cash or against services, what it may buy, and who pays bootcamp travel are not stated. The Terms & Conditions make the applying person confirm being an "EU or Norway citizen and resident", and exclude activities related to firearms and violence. The form says 23:59 CET, its confirmation text 23:59 CEST. Ask contact@eudis-business-accelerator.eu.
- **How to apply:** Read the Cohort 4 application guidelines, optionally join the webinar on 29 October 2026, fill in the ownership-and-control template, prepare the deck (and an optional two-minute video on the programme's recording platform), and submit the form (https://tally.so/r/68YyOY, linked from the homepage) before 16 November 2026 23:59 CET. Announcement and onboarding 8 January 2027; kick-off March 2027.
- **Status page:** https://www.eudis-business-accelerator.eu/ (Commission page: https://eudis.europa.eu/eudis-tracks/business-accelerator_en, still showing 2026 dates)
- **Verified:** 2026-10-17 (curl, HTTP 200; Cohort 4 guidelines PDF read with pdftotext)
- **Evidence:** "Cohort 4 call for applications is open - Apply before November 16"
- **Gotchas:** The Commission's EUDIS page still lists the 2026 spring and autumn windows, and the operator FAQ still talks about Cohort 3 with a 30 May 2026 deadline; the operator homepage, the Cohort 4 guidelines and the form are the current sources. The eligibility check is Yes/No on all four criteria before any scoring; scoring weights are defence relevance 25%, innovation 25%, problem to solution 20%, technology maturity for business 20%, team 10%. The EUR 200 000 bar and the 20-year cap rule out idea-stage and most pre-seed founders. Not open to Icelandic companies, unlike the EU-associated set the skill uses for EU doors.

## European Defence Fund open SME calls (EUDIS non-thematic research and development actions) {#edf-eudis-sme-calls}

- **Who:** SMEs (EU Recommendation 2003/361) registered in an EU Member State or Norway, with executive management there and not controlled from outside, in a consortium of at least three independent applicants from three different eligible countries with an SME as coordinator. Development topic: every partner an SME. Research topic: SMEs and non-profit research organisations, research organisations at most 40 percent of the requested grant. Single SMEs cannot apply. Iceland does not take part in the EDF.
- **What:** Two open, non-thematic calls a year for disruptive defence knowledge, products and technologies in any area of defence interest from TRL 4, including adapting civil solutions to defence. Research actions (EDF-2026-LS-DIS-RA-SMERO-NT) are funded at 100 percent for 12-36 months. Development actions (EDF-2026-LS-DA-SME-NT) are funded per activity: baseline 20 percent (prototyping) to 90 percent (studies), up to 100 percent with bonuses, with the rest co-financed. Lump-sum grants managed by the European Defence Agency; SME beneficiaries can get up to 15 days of business coaching.
- **Amount:** Research: "The requested funding cannot exceed EUR 5 000 000" (topic page). Development: "The requested funding cannot exceed EUR 6 000 000." 2026 topic budgets EUR 35 million and EUR 30 million.
- **Requirements:** SME status registered in the Participant Register; EU Member State or Norway; executive management in an eligible country; not controlled from a non-associated third country (or with an approved guarantee); consortium of three from three countries; defence products or technologies.
- **Before you apply:** 1. Consortium of at least three independent applicants from three eligible countries, SME coordinator. 2. Every beneficiary registered in the Participant Register (PIC) before submission, with the online SME self-assessment done by the deadline on accounts not older than 31/12/2024; validation by the Central Validation Service follows. 3. Ownership-control declaration from every participant, associated partner and involved subcontractor. 4. Development: co-financing declarations for costs the EDF does not cover; capability-requirement declarations from at least two Member States for design; procurement-intent declarations from at least two Member States for prototyping, testing, qualification or certification. 5. Development must include design, system prototyping, testing, qualification, certification or increasing efficiency. 6. Research: research organisations at most 40 percent of the grant, subcontracting at most 30 percent, not only studies. 7. Part B at most 50 pages (research) or 100 pages (development), with the lump-sum budget table, participant information and a list of infrastructure, facilities, assets and resources in eligible countries. 8. Submit on the Funding & Tenders Portal by 17:00 Brussels time on deadline day. 9. Financial capacity check during grant preparation for each beneficiary requesting over EUR 60 000.
- **Unclear:** No EDF work programme 2027 and no 2027 SME topic were published on 2026-10-17; ask DEFIS-EDF-PROPOSALS@ec.europa.eu or the national EDF focal point. The EUDIS page says research actions get "up to EUR 4 million", the topic page and call document say EUR 5 000 000. The security section says no grant is signed until at least one beneficiary has facility security clearance; whether that covers unclassified projects is not stated. How a startup without closed annual accounts fares in the SME self-assessment and financial capacity check is not stated.
- **How to apply:** Build the consortium, register every partner's PIC and SME self-assessment, download the forms inside the portal's Submission System (not the topic-page copies), and have the coordinating SME submit Part A online plus Part B and the annexes on the Funding & Tenders Portal before the deadline. The 2026 round ran from 11 February (development) and 15 April (research) to 29 September 2026 17:00 CET; results March 2027; grant signature by 31 December 2027.
- **Status page:** https://eudis.europa.eu/eudis-tracks/sme-calls_en (topic pages: https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-details/EDF-2026-LS-DA-SME-NT and .../EDF-2026-LS-DIS-RA-SMERO-NT, read through the portal's topicDetails JSON)
- **Verified:** 2026-10-17 (curl, HTTP 200; both call documents and the EDF list of participating countries read with pdftotext)
- **Evidence:** "Applicants need to be members of a consortium (at least three companies) based in at least three different Member States."
- **Gotchas:** Closed: the portal says the development call "closed at 17.00 on 29 September" with 54 proposals; the research call drew 279. The development topic needs co-financing declarations and, for design or prototyping-to-certification work, declarations signed by two Member States, all at submission. The research topic is funded at 100 percent and its call document asks for neither co-financing nor Member State declarations. The EUDIS FAQ phrase "at least three companies" is looser than the call document, which counts independent beneficiaries and lets research organisations take part in research actions. Ukrainian entities may already join consortia pending the EDF association agreement signed 13 July 2026.
