# Nordic public grants and loans for startups

Public, non-dilutive money for startups in Sweden, Finland, Norway, Denmark and Iceland: agency grants and state-backed loans a founder applies for directly. Every entry below was read on its primary page on 2026-10-15 unless marked otherwise; the JSON records carry only facts that appear on those pages, and each entry quotes one sentence as evidence. Re-read the status page before quoting a date or an amount, because rounds open and close and several agencies publish the next round only weeks ahead.

Not covered here: EU instruments (EIC Accelerator, Eurostars, Horizon Europe), regional funds (Regionalfonden projects run through regions and Tillväxtverket, where the applicant is usually a project owner rather than the startup), compute and cloud credits, equity instruments (Almi Invest, Tillväxtverket riskkapital), and agency calls that require a research consortium. Vinnova's call listing at vinnova.se/sok-finansiering/hitta-finansiering/ renders only with JavaScript; it was read in the in-app browser on 2026-10-15 and showed 22 current offers (filter "Aktuella nu": open and planned). Recorded below: Innovativa Startups, FFI ASP, Eureka Innowwide call 5, Språng Challenge The Baltic Marine Watchdog, Chips JU pilot-line access and the four Avancerad digitalisering calls. Seen but not recorded because a startup cannot apply on its own: calls that need a university coordinator or an academic partner (Utbildningssamverkan för nettonollutsläpp, closes 19 Oct 2026, university coordinator and two manufacturing companies; Utveckling av samarbeten inom metall- och mineralnäringen, 20 Oct 2026, research organisation plus company plus foreign actor; Framtidens innovativa lösningar inom avancerad life science, 9 Feb 2027, at least three actors with company and academia, up to 3 MSEK at 50 percent), consortium calls (NFFP-AeroX, 20 Oct 2026, aerospace, at least two organisations; ITEA, 2 Nov 2026, international software projects with a Swedish SME, up to 15 MSEK; Driving Urban Transitions, 17 Nov 2026, three countries including a municipality; FORESTPathways, 2 Dec 2026, three countries; Eureka SMART, 26 Jan 2027, two Eureka countries; Chips JU ECS calls 2026, 31 Mar 2027, European consortia, SMEs up to 450 000 EUR from Vinnova; Förberedande projekt för EU:s Raw Materials Partners, 14 Jun 2028, Nordic research coordinator) and Plastlöften för en mer hållbar plastanvändning (4 Nov 2026, at least three legal entities across a plastics value chain, up to 500 000 SEK at 100 percent). Planned, not open: Europeiskt samarbete för cell- och genterapier (November 2026) and Eurostars call 12 (December 2026, see eu-grants.md). Vinnova's "Test och Demo 2026" (2026-01600) had no open round on 2026-10-15.

## Vinnova Innovativa Startups 2026 {#vinnova-innovativa-startups}

- **Who:** Small Swedish aktiebolag, at most 10 years old at the decision date 2027-03-23, at most 10 employees (full-time equivalents), net revenue or balance sheet at most 10 MSEK in the last fiscal year (one may exceed 10 MSEK, not both), no dividend in the last fiscal year, not listed (nor its group). Companies granted funding in Innovativa Startups 2025 may not apply.
- **What:** Grant of up to 500 000 SEK for a project of at most nine months, starting in March 2027, to test and confirm the market potential of an innovative idea aimed at an international market with a sustainable business model.
- **Amount:** "Vinnova kan bevilja maximalt 500 000 kr i bidrag till projekt i denna utlysning."
- **Requirements:** Swedish AB operating in Sweden; age at most 10 years; at most 10 employees; revenue or balance sheet at most 10 MSEK; no profit distribution; not listed; no grant from the 2025 round.
- **How to apply:** Log in to Vinnova's e-service (ansok.vinnova.se, call GUID 0382e83b-...), fill in project, project parties and budget, submit before 12 Jan 2027 14:00. Info meetings 2 Nov 2026 09:00 and 9 Nov 2026 09:00. Decision 23 Mar 2027; project may start 23 Mar 2027 and must start by 30 Mar 2027.
- **Status page:** https://www.vinnova.se/e/innovativa-startups/innovativa-startups-2026
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15 (WebFetch and curl, HTTP 200)
- **Evidence:** "Utlysningen vänder sig till små, svenska aktiebolag som är högst 10 år gamla."
- **Gotchas:** Selection is partial randomisation: three expert assessors score every complete application, and among those judged to meet the criteria "i hög och likvärdig grad" the grants are decided by lottery ("lottning, så kallad randomisering"). The decision cannot be appealed. The page footer shows diarienummer 2025-04831; in Vinnova's open-data API 2016-04714 is the parent call "Innovativa Startups" and 2025-04831 is this round (open 2026-10-12, closes 2027-01-12T14:00, decision 2027-03-23), which is why the record's live block uses 2016-04714.

## Eureka Innowwide call 5 (autumn 2026) {#eureka-innowwide-call-5}

- **Who:** Innovative SMEs (EU recommendation 2003/361) in an EU member state, Iceland, Israel, Norway, Switzerland, Türkiye or the United Kingdom, legally established for at least two years by the deadline (on or before 1 December 2024) irrespective of any parent or sister company, with no funding from a previous Innowwide call (2022 to 2025), not an undertaking in difficulty. Vinnova's page: small and medium-sized companies that want to explore new international markets in Asia, Africa, Oceania or the Americas with a local partner.
- **What:** A six-month market feasibility project in a target country in Africa, the Americas, Asia or Oceania: technical, market, legal or socioeconomic assessments of an innovative solution (TRL 4 to 8) with one local partner (supplier, customer, end user, research provider or technology developer) as main subcontractor. Mono-beneficiary: only the SME signs the grant.
- **Amount:** "Upp till 60 000 euro. Medfinansiering på minst 26 000 euro krävs" (Vinnova). Eureka: fixed grant of 60 000 EUR, maximum support rate 70 percent, so total eligible costs must be at least 86 000 EUR; 70 percent (42 000 EUR) pre-financed, 30 percent (18 000 EUR) after an accepted final report. Call budget 4.2 MEUR for 70 projects.
- **Requirements:** SME in an eligible country; at least two years old at the deadline; independent subcontractor in the target country with subcontracting costs above zero; civil applications only; six-month duration; application, workplan and declarations complete in English; one application per company.
- **How to apply:** Register on Eureka's SmartSimple platform (https://eureka.smartsimple.ie/) as a person legally authorised to represent the SME, complete the form and the workplan template, invite the subcontractor to sign the commitment and declaration of honour on the platform (an email acceptance is not enough), submit before 1 December 2026 at 14:00 CET. Evaluation by two remote experts on excellence (threshold 18/30), impact (24/40) and implementation (18/30); the top 105 go to an independent panel and ethics review; results intended in the first trimester of 2027.
- **Status page:** https://www.vinnova.se/e/eureka-innowwide/eureka-innowwide-call-5/ (Eureka call page with the full rules: https://www.eurekanetwork.org/programmes-and-calls/innowwide/5th-innowwide-call-for-market-feasibility-projects-autumn-2026/)
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15 (Vinnova page curl HTTP 200; Eureka page read in the in-app browser, curl gets no TLS response)
- **Evidence:** "The applicant company must have been legally established for at least two years by the call deadline (for example, established on or before 1 December 2024), irrespective of any parent company, sister company or other affiliated entity."
- **Gotchas:** Vinnova's page has no apply button and says "Se exakta begränsningar och kriterier hos Eureka"; the money is EU partnership money paid by the Eureka Secretariat, not a Vinnova grant, and Vinnova's "beslut meddelas 20 maj 2027" is later than Eureka's "first trimester of 2027". The subcontractor is not party to the grant agreement. The target country must be outside Europe. A webinar for applicants took place on 29 September 2026 (slides on the Eureka page); Vinnova's informationsmöte was 29 September 2026 at 11:00.

## Vinnova Språng Challenge: The Baltic Marine Watchdog {#vinnova-sprang-baltic-marine-watchdog}

- **Who:** Teams with ambition, technical expertise and business drive. Only legal entities may apply (universities, research institutes, established companies, startups), headquartered in the EU, EFTA or the UK; an incorporated company must have at least 50 percent of its equity held within the EU, EFTA or the UK. Individuals and sole proprietorships can only join a team where a legal entity submits. Research teams should aim to incorporate a company.
- **What:** A two-stage challenge (10 months in total) to develop, demonstrate and validate fully autonomous systems for maritime surveillance, vessel tracking, documentation and monitoring in the Baltic Sea. Stage 1 (February to June 2027): patrol a dense-traffic area and follow a specified surface target, interim demonstration in littoral waters. Stage 2 (June to November 2027): escalated scenario with surface and underwater targets, final public demonstration in November 2027. Vinnova also offers coaching and test days.
- **Amount:** "Maximal finansiering per team: €520 000." Stage 1 price cap 170 000 EUR, Stage 2 price cap 350 000 EUR, paid as an individually fixed price based on each team's cost estimate.
- **Requirements:** Legal entity in the EU, EFTA or the UK; fully autonomous system with a safety risk assessment plan and a kill switch; the contribution must not already be financed by other public funding; every claim in the submission backed by scientific data or a declared estimate; mandatory attendance at both demonstrations (Vinnova may reclaim the stage's funds otherwise); signed Declaration of Honour.
- **How to apply:** "Ansök här" opens https://ansok.vinnova.se/utlysning/aa892961-4010-4e1c-726f-08df237ab60c?opencall=true (the online submission form in Vinnova's e-service), with the Declaration of Honour attached. Submission period 8 October to 2 December 2026 at 14:00; evaluation, pitch event and selection December 2026 to January 2027 (decision 19 January 2027); up to twelve teams enter Stage 1, up to nine continue to Stage 2; challenge closes 30 November 2027.
- **Status page:** https://www.vinnova.se/e/radikal-innovation/the-baltic-marine-watchdog-challenge/
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15 (listing read in the in-app browser; call page curl HTTP 200)
- **Evidence:** "Only legal entities are eligible to apply, such as universities, research institutes, established companies and start-ups."
- **Gotchas:** Diarienummer 2026-02609, programme Radikal innovation; the call for submissions exists only in English. The money is a fixed price for R&D services with stage milestones, not a cost-share grant. Evaluation criteria Approach, Implementation and Team weigh equally and Economy decides ties; a portfolio view on solution variety may be applied; gender balance of the team is a sub-criterion. The record has no sector rule because the schema has no maritime or robotics sector. Contact challenge@vinnova.se.

## Almi Innovationslån {#almi-innovationslan}

- **Who:** Companies registered in Sweden with up to 249 employees, new or established, with an innovation project of large potential still in its development phase.
- **What:** Loan of 50 000 to 500 000 SEK, at most 50 percent of the estimated project cost; interest set individually; repayment usually 3-5 years, at most 96 months; security usually a floating charge plus limited personal guarantee.
- **Amount:** "Innovationslån kan uppgå till minst 50 000 kronor och högst 500 000 kronor."

- **How to apply:** Prepare budget (mandatory), latest annual accounts, interim figures, liquidity forecast; "Gå till låneansökan" opens https://etjanster.almi.se/oversikt/flow/1006 (BankID). Continuous.
- **Status page:** https://www.almi.se/lan-finansiering/innovationslan/
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Du kan ansöka om Innovationslån om du driver ett företag som är registrerat i Sverige och har upp till 249 anställda."
- **Gotchas:** Almi is regional: "Alla ansökningar hanteras av ditt regionala Almi-bolag" (lan-finansiering page). Every loan is an individual assessment, so rate, security and amount vary by case and region. Typical loan around 300 000 SEK.
