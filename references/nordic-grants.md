# Nordic public grants and loans for startups

Public, non-dilutive money for startups in Sweden, Finland, Norway, Denmark and Iceland: agency grants and state-backed loans a founder applies for directly. Every entry below was read on its primary page on 2026-10-15 unless marked otherwise; the JSON records carry only facts that appear on those pages, and each entry quotes one sentence as evidence. Re-read the status page before quoting a date or an amount, because rounds open and close and several agencies publish the next round only weeks ahead.

Not covered here: EU instruments (EIC Accelerator, Eurostars, Horizon Europe), regional funds (Regionalfonden projects run through regions and Tillväxtverket, where the applicant is usually a project owner rather than the startup), compute and cloud credits, equity instruments (Almi Invest, Tillväxtverket riskkapital), and agency calls that require a research consortium. Vinnova's call listing at vinnova.se/sok-finansiering/hitta-finansiering/ renders only with JavaScript, so other open Vinnova rounds were discovered through Vinnova's open-data API (data.vinnova.se) and only the ones whose pages were read today appear as records; the API also showed open rounds for Avancerad digitalisering (four sub-calls closing 2027-01-19), Radikal innovation ("The Baltic Marine Watchdog Challenge", closing 2026-12-02) and "Framtidens innovativa lösningar inom life science" (closing 2027-02-09), whose pages were not located and which are not recorded. Vinnova's "Test och Demo 2026" (2026-01600) had no open round on 2026-10-15.

## Vinnova Innovativa Startups 2026 {#vinnova-innovativa-startups}

- **Who:** Small Swedish aktiebolag, at most 10 years old at the decision date 2027-03-23, at most 10 employees (full-time equivalents), net revenue or balance sheet at most 10 MSEK in the last fiscal year (one may exceed 10 MSEK, not both), no dividend in the last fiscal year, not listed (nor its group). Companies granted funding in Innovativa Startups 2025 may not apply.
- **What:** Grant of up to 500 000 SEK for a project of at most nine months, starting in March 2027, to test and confirm the market potential of an innovative idea aimed at an international market with a sustainable business model.
- **Amount:** "Vinnova kan bevilja maximalt 500 000 kr i bidrag till projekt i denna utlysning."
- **Requirements:** Swedish AB operating in Sweden; age at most 10 years; at most 10 employees; revenue or balance sheet at most 10 MSEK; no profit distribution; not listed; no grant from the 2025 round.
- **How to apply:** Log in to Vinnova's e-service (ansok.vinnova.se, call GUID 0382e83b-...), fill in project, project parties and budget, submit before 12 Jan 2027 14:00. Info meetings 2 Nov 2026 09:00 and 9 Nov 2026 09:00. Decision 23 Mar 2027; project may start 23 Mar 2027 and must start by 30 Mar 2027.
- **Status page:** https://www.vinnova.se/e/innovativa-startups/innovativa-startups-2026
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15 (WebFetch and curl, HTTP 200)
- **Evidence:** "Utlysningen vänder sig till små, svenska aktiebolag som är högst 10 år gamla."
- **Gotchas:** Selection is partial randomisation: three expert assessors score every complete application, and among those judged to meet the criteria "i hög och likvärdig grad" the grants are decided by lottery ("lottning, så kallad randomisering"). The decision cannot be appealed. The page footer shows diarienummer 2025-04831; in Vinnova's open-data API 2016-04714 is the parent call "Innovativa Startups" and 2025-04831 is this round (open 2026-10-12, closes 2027-01-12T14:00, decision 2027-03-23), which is why the record's live block uses 2016-04714.

## Vinnova FFI ASP - Accelerated Startup Partnership (autumn 2026) {#vinnova-ffi-asp-accelerated-startup-partnership}

- **Who:** Knowledge-intensive startups, Swedish or foreign, registered at most 10 years and meeting the EU small-enterprise definition, in a project with at least one established need owner (private or public road-transport organisation). Only the startup is beneficiary.
- **What:** Grant for an innovation collaboration accelerating sustainable road transport: validation project (TRL/BRL 2-4) up to 500 000 SEK, implementation project (TRL/BRL 5-8) up to 750 000 SEK. Vinnova can finance up to 100 percent of the startup's project costs; at least 25 percent co-financing at project level is required.
- **Amount:** "Maximum funding is SEK 500,000 for Validation Projects and SEK 750,000 for Implementation Projects."
- **Requirements:** Company registered at most 10 years; fewer than 50 employees (EU small enterprise); at least two participants with one need owner; connection to an FFI sub-programme (Circularity, Transport and mobility solutions, Traffic-safe automation, Zero emissions). Startups under five years, unlisted, no profit distributed and not formed by merger receive start-up aid; older small companies receive de minimis aid (max 300 000 EUR over three years).
- **How to apply:** "Apply here" to https://ansok.vinnova.se/utlysning/26bdd234-db2b-4fa2-1fab-08dec63ac2d3?opencall=true&lang=en with CVs and a GANTT chart; foreign startups attach a home-country registration certificate. Closes 29 Oct 2026 14:00; decision 24 Nov 2026. Reference 2026-01767.
- **Status page:** https://www.vinnova.se/en/calls-for-proposals/accelerate-swedish-partnership/ffi-asp-accelerated-startup-2026-01767/ (Swedish: https://www.vinnova.se/e/accelerera-svenskt-partnerskap/ffi-asp-accelerated-startup-2026-01767/)
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15 (WebFetch and curl, HTTP 200)
- **Evidence:** "The company must have been registered for a maximum of 10 years and meet the EU definition of "small enterprise""
- **Gotchas:** The English page is machine-translated and says the Swedish text takes precedence. The page states two "startup" definitions: the eligibility one (10 years, small enterprise) and the state-aid one (under five years) that only decides the aid basis. No country rule is encoded in the record because foreign startups may apply. The spring round 2026-00299 closed 2 June 2026; total 2026 budget 22 MSEK is shared across rounds.

## Almi Innovationslån {#almi-innovationslan}

- **Who:** Companies registered in Sweden with up to 249 employees, new or established, with an innovation project of large potential still in its development phase.
- **What:** Loan of 50 000 to 500 000 SEK, at most 50 percent of the estimated project cost; interest set individually; repayment usually 3-5 years, at most 96 months; security usually a floating charge plus limited personal guarantee.
- **Amount:** "Innovationslån kan uppgå till minst 50 000 kronor och högst 500 000 kronor."
- **Requirements:** Registered in Sweden; up to 249 employees; co-financing required (above 300 000 SEK at least half of the co-financing in capital).
- **How to apply:** Prepare budget (mandatory), latest annual accounts, interim figures, liquidity forecast; "Gå till låneansökan" opens https://etjanster.almi.se/oversikt/flow/1006 (BankID). Continuous.
- **Status page:** https://www.almi.se/lan-finansiering/innovationslan/
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Du kan ansöka om Innovationslån om du driver ett företag som är registrerat i Sverige och har upp till 249 anställda."
- **Gotchas:** Almi is regional: "Alla ansökningar hanteras av ditt regionala Almi-bolag" (lan-finansiering page). Every loan is an individual assessment, so rate, security and amount vary by case and region. Typical loan around 300 000 SEK.

## Almi Mikrolån {#almi-mikrolan}

- **Who:** Micro companies and sole traders in Sweden: at most nine employees and annual revenue or balance sheet below 2 MEUR. You must have or be about to start a company; private individuals cannot apply.
- **What:** Loan of up to 250 000 SEK for a forward-looking investment; normally no co-financing; repayment 4 to 72 months; can have longer term, initial amortisation-free period and lower rate than Almi's other loans.
- **Amount:** "Med ett Mikrolån kan du låna upp till 250 000 kronor."
- **Requirements:** Company or sole trader in Sweden; at most 9 employees; revenue or balance sheet under 2 MEUR.
- **How to apply:** Same e-service as all Almi loans: https://etjanster.almi.se/oversikt/flow/1006; a company being formed attaches budgets for the current and next fiscal year. Continuous.
- **Status page:** https://www.almi.se/lan-finansiering/mikrolan/
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Mikrolån är ett mindre lån för mikroföretag och enskilda näringsidkare i Sverige"
- **Gotchas:** The record encodes only the revenue leg of "årsomsättning eller balansomslutning som understiger två miljoner euro" because the schema has no EUR balance-sheet field. Interest normally 2-3 percentage points above bank rates. Handled by the regional Almi company.

## Almi Tillväxtlån {#almi-tillvaxtlan}

- **Who:** Innovative, established small and medium-sized aktiebolag registered in Sweden with up to 250 employees that want to grow faster than their own finances allow.
- **What:** Loan of up to 10 MSEK with an EIF guarantee under InvestEU; Almi normally finances 30 to 50 percent of the capital need; repayment 12 to 84 months; longer term, initial amortisation-free period and lower rate than standard Almi loans.
- **Amount:** "Du kan låna upp till 10 miljoner kronor med Tillväxtlån."
- **Requirements:** Aktiebolag registered in Sweden; up to 250 employees; innovative and established.
- **How to apply:** https://etjanster.almi.se/oversikt/flow/1006 with budget, annual accounts, interim figures and liquidity forecast. Continuous.
- **Status page:** https://www.almi.se/lan-finansiering/tillvaxtlan/
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Tillväxtlån är ett lån för innovativa små och medelstora aktiebolag"
- **Gotchas:** "Ett lån för etablerade och innovativa företag", so pre-revenue startups may be steered to Innovationslån or Mikrolån instead. Regional handling and individual assessment as for all Almi loans.

## Energimyndigheten - Konceptutveckling av innovationer med affärsfokus {#energimyndigheten-konceptutveckling}

- **Who:** Registered small and medium-sized companies (legal entities) and universities in Sweden with an energy-relevant innovation at TRL 2-4 and a clear business focus. "Det finns ingen åldersgräns för sökande företag – även nystartade företag kan söka."
- **What:** Grant for concept development: energy relevance, prototype or MVP, commercial potential, IP strategy; projects at most 12 months.
- **Amount:** "Sökande kan få bidrag med upp till 100 procent av stödgrundande kostnader, dock högst 500 000 kronor, det finns inget krav på samfinansiering." (programme page utveckla-en-affarside; the spring call page states only the pool "Cirka 10 miljoner kronor")
- **Requirements:** Swedish SME or university; one application per company per round; ownership or right of disposal of the solution; TRL 2-4 at start.
- **How to apply:** Mina sidor (https://minasidor.energimyndigheten.se/) with the mandatory "Ytterligare ansökningsfrågor" attachment, when a round is open.
- **Status page:** https://www.energimyndigheten.se/utlysningar/konceptutveckling-av-innovationer-med-affarsfokus/
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Sista dag för ansökan 2026-06-02 23:59"
- **Gotchas:** Closed on 2026-10-15. The call page (Senast uppdaterad 2026-04-08) still shows the spring round; decisions "löpande under september till november 2026", project start 15 Oct to 1 Dec 2026. The programme page says rounds open twice a year, "planerat april samt oktober", and the kommande-utlysningar page lists the next opening as "October 2026" with no day. Almi and five other innovation environments advise but do not write applications.

## Energimyndigheten - Verifiering av innovationer med kund {#energimyndigheten-verifiering-med-kund}

- **Who:** Small and medium-sized companies in Sweden with an energy-relevant innovation at TRL 4-7, a clear business focus and at least one customer that signs a letter of intent; no age limit.
- **What:** Grant for developing and verifying the innovation together with a customer plus business development (business plan, financing plan, IP strategy); projects up to 24 months.
- **Amount:** "Bidrag med högst 3 miljoner kronor" (programme page verifiering-med-kund, which also states co-financing "som motsvarar minst 55 procent av projektets totala kostnad"); the call page confirms the 3 MSEK maximum and a pool of "Cirka 30 miljoner kronor".
- **Requirements:** Swedish SME; maximum one application per company; ownership or right of disposal of the solution; TRL 4-7 at start; dated and signed letter of intent from a customer (Mall för avsiktsförklaring).
- **How to apply:** Mina sidor (https://minasidor.energimyndigheten.se/) with "Ytterligare ansökningsfrågor" and the letter of intent, when a round is open.
- **Status page:** https://www.energimyndigheten.se/utlysningar/verifiering-av-innovationer-med-kund/
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Sista dag för ansökan 2026-06-02 23:59"
- **Gotchas:** Closed on 2026-10-15; the call page (updated 2026-04-08) shows the spring round with decisions September to November 2026 and project start 15 Aug to 1 Dec 2026. Next opening planned October 2026 per kommande-utlysningar, no day given. Business-development activities can be funded up to 100 percent while the rest needs co-financing.

## Innovasjon Norge Startup grant 1 (Oppstartstilskudd 1) {#innovasjon-norge-oppstartstilskudd-1}

- **Who:** Norwegian private limited companies (AS) less than five years old that are genuinely new businesses with growth ambitions and have completed the idea phase; ENK, ANS and DA are not accepted; subsidiaries 50 percent or more owned by an established business are not eligible; no outstanding payment defaults.
- **What:** Grant for buying services from external suppliers that provide insight from customers and the market; project period 9 months, extendable by 3 (12 in total).
- **Amount:** "The grant can cover up to 100% of invoiced costs, up to a maximum of NOK 150,000."
- **Requirements:** AS incorporated before applying; less than five years old; genuinely new business.
- **How to apply:** Application deadline "Ongoing". The page has no apply button; it links "Get feedback on your project" (raskavklaring.innovasjonnorge.no) and mentions "My page" for reporting. The Startup grant 2 page links My page at https://start.innovasjonnorge.no/.
- **Status page:** https://www.innovasjonnorge.no/en/tjeneste/oppstartstilskudd-1
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15 (WebFetch; curl with browser UA returns HTTP 403)
- **Evidence:** "The grant only covers the purchase of services from external suppliers."
- **Gotchas:** Up to 50 percent of the grant is paid at project start and the rest at completion; "At least 50% of the project costs must be paid upfront". Own staff costs, related-party costs, VAT and pre-application costs are not covered. The application must disclose other public funding sought.

## Innovasjon Norge Startup grant 2 (Oppstartstilskudd 2) {#innovasjon-norge-oppstartstilskudd-2}

- **Status page:** https://www.innovasjonnorge.no/en/tjeneste/oppstartstilskudd-2

## Business Finland Sprint Grant {#business-finland-sprint}

- **Who:** Finnish limited liability companies with a Finnish Business ID, fewer than 50 employees and turnover or balance sheet at most 10 MEUR, a committed team of at least two people in Finland and at least 50 000 EUR of equity financing on the balance sheet, developing innovative solutions for international markets. Aimed at companies under five years without earlier Business Finland funding; older companies must show a significant innovation-based change of direction.
- **What:** De minimis grant for a project where at least two thirds of costs are R&D; operational, sales, marketing and production investments excluded.
- **Amount:** "covers 75% of the project's eligible costs, meaning that a company can apply for funding of 75,000–100,000 euros."
- **Requirements:** Finnish Oy; fewer than 50 employees; turnover or balance sheet at most 10 MEUR; team of two in Finland; 50 000 EUR equity.
- **How to apply:** Register in My Business Finland, book a meeting with a Business Finland expert, then submit in the Funding e-service (https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/services/funding/funding-e-service/) after the meeting.
- **Status page:** https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/services/funding/calls/2026/ongoing-sprint-grant-funding/
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Continuous call from September 1, 2026."
- **Gotchas:** Status "Ongoing", decisions continuous, page updated 21.8.2026. Tempo is gone from the Startup funding page, which now lists Sprint, R&D funding and the Deep Tech Accelerator. The balance-sheet alternative and the equity requirement are not encoded as rules.

## Business Finland Research, development and piloting funding {#business-finland-rd-funding}

- **Who:** Companies registered in Finland regardless of legal form (not natural persons, sole traders or companies in difficulty under EU state-aid rules), with a permanent place of business in Finland, a Finnish business ID, a committed team of at least two people in Finland and the means to cover costs before payment.
- **What:** Loans for development and pilot projects, grants for research projects.
- **Amount:** "Loans are granted for development projects (including pilot projects) at 50% of the project's eligible costs." SMEs and mid-caps may get 70 percent where justified; research grants up to 50 percent for SMEs, 40 percent for mid-cap and large companies. "The amount of funding to be granted is set in the Government's annual budget."
- **Requirements:** Finnish registration and business ID; team of two in Finland; ability to pre-finance.
- **How to apply:** Funding e-service; the form can only be created by a person who may represent the company alone per the Trade Register or who holds the Suomi.fi authorisation "Applying for corporate financing".
- **Status page:** https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/services/funding/calls/2026/research-development-and-piloting/
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Applications are processed continuously."
- **Gotchas:** Status "Ongoing", updated 22.9.2026. The record's kind is loan because development and piloting, the usual startup case, are loan-funded; research projects are grants. Funding is "competitive and discretionary".

## Business Finland Deep Tech Accelerator (DTA) {#business-finland-deep-tech-accelerator}

- **Who:** Finnish limited liability companies less than five years old at the first-phase decision, fewer than 50 employees, net sales or balance sheet at most 10 MEUR, at least one full financial year, at least 10 percent of total costs on R&D in one of the previous three years, in the prepayment and employer registers, with self-financing for the whole phase.

- **Requirements:** As above; total financing for phase 1 secured before the decision.
- **How to apply:** Funding e-service, Young Innovative Company template, with appendices, before the call deadline.
- **Status page:** https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/services/funding/calls/2026/dta-funding-call-2026/
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Submit the application and its appendices via Business Finland's online service by 16 August 2026 at 11:59 PM"
- **Gotchas:** Closed on 2026-10-15: the 2026 call shows status "Ended" with period "11.5.-16.8.2026"; money for phase 1 had to be in the account by 2 September 2026. The DTA page says one call per year and that upcoming calls will be announced there; no next date. Costs are accepted until the company turns eight; the phase 3 loan has a 30 percent advance. The call page contains an odd sentence about the Trade Register date "27 November 2026" that looks like an error.
