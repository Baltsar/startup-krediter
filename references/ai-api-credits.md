# AI API and GPU-cloud credits

Credits from model providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Mistral) and GPU-cloud vendors (Together AI, Modal, Lambda, Runpod, Hugging Face, Replicate) are a different door from EuroHPC or national GPU-hour allocations: these are vendor-funded balances that only work inside that vendor's own console, and EuroHPC hours never turn into API credits or vice versa. Almost every program here is partner-locked (a VC in the vendor's network, or a program such as NVIDIA Inception) or cohort-based, so "open" changes month to month; the Verified line and the notes say what was true on 2026-10-15, and the status page must be re-read before quoting an amount or saying a door is open. Not covered: hyperscaler programs (AWS Activate, Google for Startups Cloud, Microsoft for Startups), which live in the cloud-credits domain even when they bundle partner-model credits; free tiers and promo codes from perk marketplaces; and anything that could only be found on third-party aggregator sites. A vendor page that could not be fetched (bot block) or that shows no program gets an entry below but no record in the JSON.

## Claude Startups (Anthropic) {#claude-startups}

- **Who:** Early-stage founders building on Claude. Founders backed by a VC in Anthropic's partner network can get additional API credits through that VC.
- **What:** Membership program: discounts and credits worth up to $45,000 from companies building with Claude (the Startup Stack, redeemed by code in the Claude Console), Applied AI group sessions every other week, events, hackathons and meetups. Up to $100K in additional Claude API credits for founders backed by a partner-network VC, claimed through the VC.
- **Amount:** Up to 45 000 USD in partner offers and credits; up to 100 000 USD in additional Claude API credits via a partner VC.
- **Requirements:** Builds on the first-party Claude API. No funding caps, tiers or work-email requirement are published. The 100K route requires a VC in Anthropic's partner network.
- **How to apply:** Applications are closed. Subscribe to the Claude Startup newsletter on the status page to hear when the next cohort opens, then apply there. Member sign-in: platform.claude.com/claude-for-startups.
- **Status page:** https://claude.com/programs/startups
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Our current cohort is now full, and we're not accepting new applications right now."
- **Gotchas:** Cohort full on 2026-10-15; applications still in review were not advanced and those founders were emailed. Credits apply only to the first-party Claude API through the Claude Console and cannot be used on AWS, Google Cloud, Azure or other third-party platforms. The 45K figure is partner offers, not Anthropic API credits.

## OpenAI for Startups {#openai-for-startups}

- **Who:** Startups backed by a VC in OpenAI's partner network; the FAQ names Thrive Capital, Sequoia, a16z, Kleiner Perkins and Conviction Partners. Founders without a partner VC get events, Build Hours and code repositories, not credits.
- **What:** Free API credits, rate-limit upgrades, time with solutions engineers, invitations to events and a direct line to the Startup team, all unlocked through the partner VC.
- **Amount:** Not published on the page.
- **Requirements:** A unique referral code from the partner VC; a description of the product and how it uses the API; company name and contacts with a valid business email; basic funding details.
- **How to apply:** Ask the investor whether they are in the network and for the referral code, then submit the credit application with the items above. The founder clicks submit.
- **Status page:** https://openai.com/startups/
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15, read in a browser (the site returns 403 to automated fetch and redirects to the visitor's locale, here sv-SE).
- **Evidence:** "Kontakta ditt riskkapitalbolag för att få veta hur du kan få tillgång till OpenAI API-krediter."
- **Gotchas:** Entirely partner-locked; the page lists no amount, deadline or cohort. When credits run out, the account switches to standard billing automatically. The FAQ also offers zero data retention on paid plans on request.

## Together AI Startup Accelerator {#together-ai-startup-accelerator}

- **Who:** Startups selected into the accelerator; tier by funding raised: Build (up to $5M raised), Scale ($5M to $10M raised), Grow (over $10M raised).
- **What:** Free platform credits for serverless inference, dedicated endpoints, fine-tuning and instant clusters; free forward-deployed engineering hours (3, 6 or 10 by tier); exclusive content; joint GTM; community events; access to Together's VC network.
- **Amount:** Up to 15 000 USD (Build), 30 000 USD (Scale) or 50 000 USD (Grow) in platform credits.
- **Requirements:** Builds on Together AI. Selection-based; no partner-VC, work-email or funding-cap requirement is stated.
- **How to apply:** Fill in the "Submit your application" form at the bottom of the status page; the founder clicks submit.
- **Status page:** https://www.together.ai/startup-accelerator
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "A targeted selection-based program for startups."
- **Gotchas:** Credits do not apply to Reserved GPU Clusters. Testimonials mention an "inaugural cohort", so intake may be cohort-based even though the form was live on 2026-10-15. Amounts are "up to", decided at selection.

## Modal for Startups {#modal-for-startups}

- **Who:** VC-funded startups new to Modal. Seed to Series A: raised any amount from Modal's VC partner network, or over $1M VC funding from any fund. Scaling and Series B+: raised over $30M or post-Series B, with a VC in the partner network (custom package).
- **What:** One-time grant of free Modal compute credits ("thousands of free GPU credits"), direct access to Modal's engineering team, GTM amplification on launches and raises, founder community.
- **Amount:** Not published; the page says only "thousands of free GPU credits", assigned by tier.
- **Requirements:** New to Modal; VC funding as above; a payment method on the account (per FAQ question). Partner logos shown: Y Combinator, Andreessen Horowitz, Sequoia, Khosla Ventures, Neo, HF0, Pear VC, Lux Capital.
- **How to apply:** Click "Apply Now" on the status page and fill in the embedded Fillout "Startup credits form" at https://modal.com/startups#apply; the founder clicks submit.
- **Status page:** https://modal.com/startups
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Customers that are new to Modal and meet the below requirements are eligible to apply. Credits are a one-time grant and you may not reapply once you have received credits in a given tier."
- **Gotchas:** Form live on 2026-10-15. The FAQ answers (credit duration and expiry, tier assignment, where to see credit status, full VC partner list, why a payment method is required) are rendered client-side and were not readable in the fetch; open the FAQ in a browser before promising a duration. The "$30 / month free compute" banner is a general offer, not part of this program. A separate Modal for Academics program exists.

## Lambda Cloud credits for NVIDIA Inception members {#lambda-nvidia-inception}

- **Who:** Companies that are members of NVIDIA Inception, located in and paying with a credit card from a listed country: Australia, Canada, Chile, European Union (except Lithuania), Iceland, Israel, Japan, New Zealand, Norway, Puerto Rico, Singapore, South Africa, South Korea, Switzerland, Taiwan, Turkey, UAE, UK, US.
- **What:** 1 000 USD in Lambda Cloud credits for on-demand GPU instances (NVIDIA B200, H100, A100, GH200 Grace Hopper), no quotas or GPU tiers, no ingress or egress fees; credits cover persistent storage at $0.20/GB/month.
- **Amount:** 1 000 USD in Lambda Cloud credits.
- **Requirements:** NVIDIA Inception membership (hard requirement the schema cannot express as a rule); one grant per company; valid credit card on file.
- **How to apply:** Fill in the HubSpot form on the status page; the founder clicks submit. Join NVIDIA Inception first if needed.
- **Status page:** https://lambda.ai/nvidia-inception
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Get $1,000 in cloud credits to build AI with on-demand GPU instances on Lambda Cloud:"
- **Gotchas:** Applications are reviewed and approved by Lambda; submitting does not guarantee credits. Credits are valid for 12 months from the date applied. Lithuania is excluded. Third-party sites quote 7 500 USD for this offer; the page says 1 000 USD today. No general "Lambda for Startups" credit page exists on lambda.ai as of 2026-10-15 (the "Startups and Researchers" nav item links to the instances pricing page).

## Runpod Startup Program {#runpod-startup-program}

- **Who:** AI startups new to Runpod with real GPU workloads. Starter tier: pre-Series A startups with working products and near-term GPU needs. Growth tier: venture-backed Seed to Series B+ startups with production workloads, ready to commit $50K upfront.
- **What:** Credits usable across Pods, Serverless, Clusters, Public Endpoints and Storage. Starter: $1,000 in free credits, self-serve onboarding, direct support. Growth: commit $50,000 upfront under a 12-month agreement and Runpod adds $25,000 in bonus credits ($75,000 total), plus a 30-minute 1:1 onboarding call, SLA options and an enterprise path.
- **Amount:** Starter 1 000 USD free; Growth 25 000 USD bonus credits on a 50 000 USD commitment.
- **Requirements:** Builds on Runpod. Venture backing is "strongly preferred", not required: the FAQ says every application is reviewed holistically. Primarily for new customers.
- **How to apply:** Click "Apply now" on the status page and fill in the form (funding status, workload type, timing, commitment); the founder clicks submit.
- **Status page:** https://www.runpod.io/startup-program
- **Verified:** 2026-10-15
- **Evidence:** "Commit $50K upfront and we'll add $25K in bonus credits to your account — you start with $75K total."
- **Gotchas:** Selective; not every applicant is accepted. The Growth tier is a deposit match, not free money: $50K of your own cash is locked into a 12-month agreement, and the $25K bonus is one-time with no reload. Best-fit guidance on the page: Growth for startups spending or ready to spend $5K to $25K+ per month on GPUs. Existing customers go through their account manager. Separate Creator Program and academic research offer exist.
