## The Delaware applicant is the problem

Your GO belongs to Fjordline Robotics Inc., a US company. The EIC only accepts a full proposal from a company established in the EU or a Horizon Europe associated country, and the US is neither.

- **The rule:** the Work Programme 2026 lets a company from outside those countries send the short proposal. By the full proposal it "must prove its effective establishment in a Member State or an Associated Country".
- **The EIC FAQ** says a related company in the EU can apply in its own right. The non-EU company that sent the short proposal must itself be established in the EU by the full proposal.
- **Switching to the AB is not clearly allowed.** The Work Programme's footnote on these cases says "the applicant must use the same identification (PIC number) when submitting the full application".
- **The equity has the same snag.** The EIC Fund invests only in companies established in the EU or an associated country, or in their parent if the parent is too (EIC Fund Investment Guidelines, 27 August 2026). With the Inc. on top, the 6M EUR would go into the AB, below YC and your seed fund. The FAQ also warns that an EU group company must not be used to get around the eligibility rules for blended finance.

As the rules read today, a full proposal from the Inc. on 4 November would be ineligible. Only the EIC can say whether the AB can use your GO.
