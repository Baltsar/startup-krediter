## 1. Program

**Fyrirtækjastyrkur Sproti** (English: "Sprout grant for companies") from Tækniþróunarsjóður (the Technology Development Fund), administered by Rannís. It has not been renamed. For autumn 2026 the process was redesigned into two stages: a pre-stage (forþrep) with nine Lean Canvas-style screening questions, then a main stage (aðalþrep) for invited applicants.

The hidden profile does not rule the founder out:
- **Age:** Hafbók ehf. was registered on 11 March 2024, so it is 2 years 11 months old at a 15 February 2027 deadline.
- **Size and ownership:** it is an SME, and no company owns 25% or more of it.
- **Running grants:** it holds no active grant, because Fræ finished in 2025.
- **De minimis:** 2M + 24M ISK is about EUR 191,000, under the EUR 300,000 cap.
- **Financial difficulty:** the test (rules 8.2) does not apply. It covers non-de minimis grants only, and equity is positive anyway (0.5M + 25M − 9M ISK).

So there is no "next step" program. Fit is conditional on the maturity stage (question 1) and on timing.

## 2. Stopper list

### The trap

The Sproti page shows status "Lokað fyrir umsóknir", application period 04.08.2026 to 15.09.2026, and no next date. The rules (2.7) set yearly deadlines on 15 February and 15 September at 15:00.

The official chain from deadline to cash:
1. The stage-1 result comes within three months of the stage-1 deadline (evaluation page).
2. Only A1/A2 applications may submit the full stage-2 application (Sproti page).
3. The board decides, and the contract meeting is held within two months of the award notice (rules 5.7).
4. 40% is paid at contract signing (rules 6.4).
5. Only costs incurred after the deadline are eligible (rules 4.1).

The last spring round gives a real-world benchmark. That call opened on 5 January 2026 and closed on 16 February 2026. The board's allocation, Sproti included, was announced on 2 June 2026, and that round was still single-stage.

So no Sproti money can reach Hafbók before its cash runs out on 31 January 2027. Realistically, the first payment comes in the second half of 2027. The only Tækniþróunarsjóður line open all year is Fræ/Þróunarfræ, with a maximum of 2M ISK (page 1-42).

**Neither conversation asked when the founders need the money. Neither said that Sproti cash cannot come before the runway ends, and neither mentioned Fræ/Þróunarfræ as the only all-year line.** Both gave fragments: the 3-month stage-1 result and the 40/40/20 payment schedule. Neither drew the conclusion that matters to this founder.
