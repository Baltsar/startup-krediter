## 1. Program

The founder asked for Business Finland's Tempo. Tempo closed on 3.10.2025, and Business Finland announced on 21.01.2026 that it will not reopen. Its successor today is the **Business Finland Sprint grant (Sprint-avustus)**: a continuous call from 1.9.2026, a 75% de minimis grant of 75,000-100,000 EUR on a project of 100,000-133,334 EUR.

## 2. Stopper list

### The rule that rules the founder out

The founder's company was registered on 2023-03-15, so it is 3 years 7 months old. It received ESA BIC Finland incubation funding in 2025.

The Sprint call lists this as a barrier: "Hakija on alle 5-vuotias yritys ja sille on myönnetty aiemmin jokin seuraavista Business Finlandin rahoituksista: Sprint, Tempo (tai tätä vastaava rahoitus yrityshautomo-osallistumiseen, esim. ESA-BIC), T&K-rahoitus, Nuoret Innovatiiviset Yritykset tai Deep Tech Accelerator." In English: the applicant is under 5 years old and was earlier granted one of these Business Finland fundings, and ESA-BIC is named as Tempo's equivalent.

Two more rules close the same door:
- **Target group.** For companies under 5, the call is for startups "jotka eivät ole aiemmin saaneet Business Finlandin rahoitusta", that is, with no earlier Business Finland funding. The ESA BIC call page says companies "apply simultaneously for Business Finland's 'Accelerators and preparation of international projects' funding". At least part of ESA BIC money is therefore Business Finland money.
- **Same solution, any age.** Sprint is also barred if any of the listed fundings went to the same solution. Waiting until the company turns 5 on 15.3.2028 therefore does not reopen Sprint for the forest-damage solution, and a company of 5 or older also needs a significant innovation-based change of direction.

The trap holds: the founder is ruled out of Sprint.

### The next step the funder points to

Business Finland's startup page names, for companies under 5, **Research, development and piloting funding (T&K-rahoitus)** alongside Sprint. The third option it lists, Deep Tech Accelerator, "is not the right option" without a completed T&K project.

The T&K call has no age limit and no Tempo or ESA-BIC barrier. Its gate for this founder is the undertaking-in-difficulty test.
- The Business Finland pages state that test only as "lost more than half of its subscribed share capital due to accumulated losses", applied to companies 3 years or older.
- Under the EU definition, which those pages refer to (GBER Art. 2(18)(a)), losses are deducted from reserves and other own funds first, and SVOP is such a reserve. I could not fetch the regulation text today.
- On that method, 320,000 EUR SVOP minus 120,000 EUR losses leaves positive equity: 202,500 EUR including the share capital. The company is therefore not in difficulty, and T&K is open to it.

## 4. Untraceable figures

- **A:** "de minimis -tukea 3 vuodessa enintään 225 000 EUR" (de minimis aid in 3 years at most 225,000 EUR). No official page prints 225,000. The Sprint terms set a 300,000 EUR ceiling over 3 years, and 225,000 is that ceiling minus the 75,000 EUR minimum grant. The figure is consistent as an eligibility test but unexplained, and a reader could mistake it for the ceiling. It does not affect this founder, whose de minimis total is 50,000 EUR.
- **B:** none.
