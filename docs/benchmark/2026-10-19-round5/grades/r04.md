# r04 grade: Almi Innovationslån, 500k for a v2 prototype

All program facts below come from official Almi pages that I fetched today, 2026-10-19, with curl using a browser User-Agent. Every page returned HTTP 200:
- almi.se/lan-finansiering/innovationslan/
- the Swedish and English innovation-loan FAQs

- the public pre-login overview of the e-service, etjanster.almi.se/oversikt/overview/345

The only non-Almi page I checked was Lund University's IP guidance, to see whether A's claim about it was plausible. Third-party sources are not used as evidence.

## 1. Program

The program is Almi Innovationslån (English: Innovation Loan), from Almi Företagspartner. It is live with rolling intake: "Till låneansökan" goes to the e-service and no deadline is published. The terms:
- Amount: 50,000 to 500,000 SEK, "i många fall" around 300,000.
- Almi finances at most 50% of the estimated project cost.
- Above 300,000 SEK, at least half of the co-financing must be capital. Own work may count only "vid lägre lånebelopp ... i vissa fall".

The founder is not ruled out, so there is no next-step program. The hidden profile fits fully for up to 400,000 SEK.

## 3. Factual errors

**A:** none found. Everything A presents as fact matches the official pages:
- amounts, the 50% rule and the capital rule
- the typical 300k loan
- collateral, interest and repayment (3–5 years, maximum 96 months)
- processing time (3–4 weeks)
- BankID, kundkännedom, credit check, residence condition, no deadline, not binding
- applying before registration, no lending for aktiekapital

A's e-service step names (Ansökan lån, Verksamhet, Ekonomi, Företagsuppgifter, then preview and "Signera och skicka in") match Almi's public e-service overview exactly. A gives 249 employees while the e-service and forbered-detta pages say 250. Almi's own pages disagree, so this is not an error.

**B:** one minor error. Its list of what to have ready opens with "Ett aktiebolag registrerat i Sverige". Almi says "ett företag som är registrerat i Sverige", and its guarantee FAQ speaks of the case "när ett aktiebolag ansöker", which implies other forms can apply. This makes no difference to this founder, who has an AB.

B also writes "Kapitalkravet är detsamma som vid 300k". That is loose wording: at exactly 300k the over-300k capital rule does not formally apply. The practical point holds anyway, since all 200k of angel money is needed to cover cash costs. The rest of B matches the official pages:
- interest 2–3 percentage points over banks
- collateral, 3–5 years and 96 months, 3–4 weeks, no appeal
- the advisor-led process
- not binding, no payout before registration

## 4. Untraceable figures

**A:** the turn-1 status card lists "yngre än 5 år, eller stöd av mindre betydelse på 3 år under 300 000 EUR" as an eligibility condition for Innovationslån. No Innovationslån or general Almi loan page states it. The 300,000 EUR de minimis ceiling appears only on Almi's Verifieringsmedel page, which is a different program. A partly walks it back: in turn 1 under "Oklart" ("Sidan anger ingen åldersgräns") and in turn 2 ("Almis sidor sätter i dag ingen gräns för tidigare stöd"). The founder passes either way (company from 2024, 100k SEK de minimis).

**B:** the 250 kr/hour value for own work, and the example derived from it (600 h × 250 = 150k, budget 650k, cap 325k). It comes from Driva Eget, a third-party source that B labels as old and secondary while saying Almi's pages give no figure. B's timeline (decision late November, money in December, 1–2 months) is labelled as its own estimate built from the official 3–4 weeks, so I do not count it. A has no comparable estimate.

## 5. Best recommendation: B

The truth is that the founder qualifies for up to 400,000 SEK: an 800k project, the angel's 200k as capital, and their own work for the rest.

B asked the right three questions in turn 1 (total budget, available capital, registered AB). In turn 2 it then:
- said plainly that 500k does not work
- computed a correct floor and ceiling. At 300k, the 500k of cash costs equals Almi 300k plus angel 200k. At 400k, Almi 400k + angel 200k + own work 200k = 800k, with 200k of 400k co-financing as capital.
- told the founder not to inflate the budget
- recommended applying for 400k now, with 300k as the stated minimum
- flagged the open question of own work above 300k
- checked the timeline against the founder's need for money before the season

That is exactly the right recommendation.

A stated the same rules, but its turn-1 questions were about legal form, prior aid and university ties, so it never learned the budget or the capital. In turn 2 it builds the application on 500k with a project cost of "minst 1 000 000 SEK" and at least 250k of capital. This founder cannot meet that. A does add the conditional that an 800k budget gives at most 400k.

## 6. Draft: A (B was not asked)

Only the founder in conversation A asked for a draft ("ja gärna, kör!" after A offered). The founder in B never asked, so this field must not count against B.

A's draft has real strengths:
- It follows the official e-service steps in order.
- It covers the innovation questions on "Så fungerar Almis lån": customer need, how found, how solved today, what is new, results.
- It lists the attachments correctly, including the four-month rule.
- It applies the 50% cap and the capital rule correctly in its financing table.
- It uses only facts the founder gave and marks every gap with [FYLL I].

Its usefulness is limited because it is anchored at 500k on a budget of at least 1 MSEK, which the hidden profile cannot support. Most fields are still placeholders. It is not a draft for a program the founder is ruled out of: only the amount is wrong.

## 8. Verdict: B

B gives this founder the accurate answer that matters: 500k is impossible with an 800k project, and 400k fits, with the angel's 200k exactly meeting the over-300k capital rule. It recommends the right amount and lines the timeline up with the February 2027 deadline. A covers slightly more stoppers and produces a well-structured, honest draft in Almi's real e-service order. But that draft asks for 500k on a 1 MSEK budget the founder does not have, and A presents an untraceable age/de minimis eligibility condition. B's misses (size limits, residence, state aid) do not bite for this founder. A's mis-anchored amount would have to be redone before submission.
