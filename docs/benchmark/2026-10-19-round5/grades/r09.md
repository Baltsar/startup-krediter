## 1. Program

SkatteFUNN (Skattefradrag for forskning og utvikling i et nyskapende næringsliv), run by Norges forskningsråd with Skatteetaten. The 2026 call is active, with rolling submission ("Søknadsfrist: Løpende") and a guarantee deadline of 1 Sep 2026. It has not been renamed or replaced. The government has proposed changes from income year 2027, and Stortinget has not passed them yet.

## 2. Stopper list

The hidden profile rules the founder out of approval today. The AS was registered 18 May 2021, more than three years before any 2026 decision. Its 2025 accounts show share capital plus share premium of 6,030,000 NOK, and its accumulated loss of 4,100,000 NOK is more than half of that (3,015,000). Forskningsrådet's own page says share premium counts the same as share capital in this test. That makes the company a *foretak i vanskeligheter* (FIV), and SkatteFUNN cannot approve it. The trap is confirmed.

The rule cannot be waived, but it can be fixed. The funder's pages point to a capital increase or reduction that is actually carried out and shown in a mellombalanse dated before approval. They do not point to another program, so there is no next-step program.

1. **Not a company in difficulty when Forskningsrådet approves.** A company over three years old is FIV when accumulated losses exceed half of share capital plus share premium. The test uses the last approved annual accounts, or a mellombalanse dated between those accounts and the approval. A decided capital change counts if it is actually carried out. The company must sign a self-declaration (egenerklæring) that it is not FIV. (https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/statstottereglene/vansker/ ; https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/hvem/ ; call page "Dette kreves for å få skattefradrag")
2. **Timing.** Only applications sent by 1 Sep are guaranteed processing in the same year. Later ones are handled in date order without a guarantee. Costs before approval count only if they fall in the same calendar year as the approval. An application approved in 2027 therefore loses all 2026 costs. (https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/sok/ ; https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/vurdering/ ; FAQ ; Skatte-ABC F-29-5.2)
3. **Applicant.** The company must be registered in Brønnøysundregistrene with an organisation number and be taxable in Norway, or the application is rejected. (https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/hvem/)
4. **The project must be R&D.** It needs an R&D challenge with no known solution, a systematic method that produces new knowledge, and work packages that count as industrial research or experimental development. Routine product development, operations and customer work are excluded. (call page "Vurderingskriterier" ; https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/vurdering/)
5. **Targeted and bounded.** One project per application, with concrete, measurable goals and a plan and budget split into work packages. (https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/sok/ ; https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/vurdering/)
6. **Mandatory roles.** The project manager and the organisation representative must have a Min side profile, verified by e-mail and SMS, and must accept the role invitation (within 7 days) before the application can be sent. (call page ; https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/roller/ ; FAQ)
7. **Timesheets.** Every hour must be logged daily and signed at least quarterly by the person and the project manager. This also applies to hours worked before approval, and the funder calls it an "absolutt krav". Hours without compliant timesheets give no deduction. This matters here because the project has run since January 2026. (https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/godkjenning/prosjektregnskap/ ; FAQ ; Skatteetaten SkatteFUNN page)
8. **Personnel cost rule.** Hours are costed at 1.2 per mille of agreed and actual annual salary, capped at 700 NOK/hour and 1,850 hours per person per year. Unpaid work does not count. (https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/godkjenning/prosjektregnskap/)
9. **Auditor.** A revisor must attest the project accounts and sign them in Altinn, even if the company has opted out of audit. (prosjektregnskap page ; call page ; Skatteetaten SkatteFUNN page)
10. **Reporting.** A status report or final report must reach Forskningsrådet by 1 March for the year the claim covers. (call page ; https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/godkjenning/rapportering/)
11. **Claim.** The deduction must be claimed in the tax return (skattemelding) with næringsspesifikasjon by 31 May. (call page ; https://www.skatteetaten.no/nn/bedrift-og-organisasjon/skatt-for-bedrift-og-organisasjon/skattemelding-for-naringsdrivande/fradrag/forskning-og-gave/skattefunn-ordninga/)
12. **Project window in the 2026 call.** The project may start no earlier than 1 Jan 2026 and no later than 31 Dec 2026. Costs from 2025 cannot be included. (call page)
13. **Other public aid to the same project.** Aid-intensity caps can cut or remove the SkatteFUNN deduction. Grants from Innovasjon Norge and others must be applied for before the SkatteFUNN project starts. (https://www.forskningsradet.no/skattefunn/statstottereglene/Insentiveffekt/ ; call page "Statsstøtte")

**Count:** A statement that is partial but correct counts. One framed in a way that misleads on this founder's situation does not.

- **A states 11 of 13:** items 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 and 13.
  - A never mentions the FIV rule (item 1), and never asks anything that would surface it.
  - On item 7, A only says "Før timelister og eget prosjektregnskap" under "Etter godkjenning". That misses the point that matters here: hours already worked in 2026 need signed timesheets now. So item 7 is not counted.
- **B states 12 of 13:** every item except 11.
  - B asked the FIV question in turn 1 and applied the rule correctly in turn 2.
  - For item 11, B says how the deduction is paid out but never that it must be claimed in the skattemelding. Its only mention of RF-1053 is tied to the auditor's attestation.

## 3. Factual errors

None found in either conversation. I checked these against the official pages:
- **Both:** the 19% rate, the 25 MNOK cap (B adds 4.75 MNOK, which is correct), the 1.2 per mille / 700 NOK / 1,850-hour rule, the 1 Sep guarantee, the same-year rule for pre-approval costs, the 1–48-month duration and the 1 Jan / 31 Dec 2026 start window, the 1 March reports, the 2027 proposals being unpassed, and the 30 Nov webinar.
- **A only:** 31 May for the skattemelding, AS tax settlements from late August to late November, payout shortly after the settlement, a registered account number being needed, and offsetting against tax arrears.
- **B only:** the FIV arithmetic (6,030,000 / 3,015,000 / 4,100,000 / 1,930,000), the minimum capital increase of 2,170,000 NOK plus twice the year-to-date loss, and RF-1053 as the SkatteFUNN form.

## 4. Untraceable figures

No amount, date or open-status claim in either conversation was untraceable.

One rule claim in A could not be traced: "Overgangsreglene som er omtalt, gjelder prosjekter som allerede er godkjent". It comes from EY, a third party. Forskningsrådet's page on the 2027 proposals says nothing about transition rules, and regjeringen.no could not be read. B marks the same point as unclear and names its source (CMS), which is the more honest handling.

## 5. Best recommendation: B

- **A** tells the founder to send the application this week and says they have "en reell sjanse for godkjenning i 2026". Given the hidden profile, they cannot be approved, or would have to sign a false self-declaration, until the equity is restored.
- **B** finds the stopper, does the arithmetic, names the official fix and says it is urgent if 2026 costs are to count.

B could have been sharper on timing. The FIV test applies at approval, not at submission, so the founder could also secure a place in the queue now while the capital is fixed. Even so, B's recommendation is much closer to right.

## 6. Draft: tie

The founder asked for drafting help in B, so this item applies, but neither conversation contains a draft that could be submitted. B declined because the company currently cannot be approved, and offered to write the application once the capital is fixed. The rubric says not to draft for a program the hidden profile rules the founder out of, so that refusal was correct. In A the founder never asked. B's correct refusal is already credited under best recommendation, A3 and the verdict, so I score this item a tie rather than count it twice.

## 8. Verdict: B

B finds the one rule that blocks this founder: the company is over three years old and has lost more than half of share capital plus premium. B explains it with the correct arithmetic and the official fix, and correctly refuses to draft until the fix is in place.
