# Grade r03 (grader g2)

Date of checks: 2026-10-19. All evidence comes from official pages fetched today with curl (browser User-Agent), pdftotext, and one browser tab for the JavaScript-only EuroHPC access portal and its public API.

## 1. Program

The founder asked about "SNIC", meaning Berzelius or Alvis. SNIC was replaced by **NAISS (National Academic Infrastructure for Supercomputing in Sweden)** on 1 January 2023 (KTH/PDC news, https://www.pdc.kth.se/about/pdc-news/naiss-rounds-for-compute-storage-allocations-in-2023-open-1.1218879).

- NAISS allocates compute in SUPR rounds: Small, Medium, Large and LUMI.
- Alvis is listed in the NAISS Small 2026 round as "not possible to apply for ... as it will be decommissioned". The C3SE home page marks it "(retired)".
- Berzelius is not a NAISS system. It is a KAW donation to NSC, applied for in a separate SUPR round, "LiU Berzelius 2026".

**Next step that the funder's own pages point to:**
- The SUPR "EuroHPC Calls" page (https://supr.naiss.se/round/eurohpc/) says the Swedish shares of Arrhenius and LUMI are "limited to applicants from Swedish academia". It sends everyone else to EuroHPC calls for the European shares.
- Sweden AI Factory, the former Mimer AI Factory coordinated by NAISS/LiU and RISE (https://swedenaifactory.se/about/faq/), sends companies to the EuroHPC AI Factories industrial-innovation calls.
- For 20–30k GPU-h, that call is **EuroHPC JU AI Factories Fast Lane Access**. The open call is EHPC-AIF-2026FL.

**Chosen program (NAISS, with the Alvis and Berzelius rounds)**

1. NAISS compute is free only to researchers at Swedish universities and research institutes. A company cannot be the applicant, and the Swedish shares of Arrhenius and LUMI are academic only. **A, B**. Sources: https://www.naiss.se/allocations/, https://user.naiss.se/allocations/, https://supr.naiss.se/round/eurohpc/
2. Small: the PI must be at least a PhD student and hold an appointment of at least 20% at a Swedish university or VR-recognised institute. **A, B** (both state the rank only, not the 20% appointment). Sources: https://user.naiss.se/allocations/small/, https://supr.naiss.se/round/2026small/
3. Medium and Large: the PI must be at least an assistant professor with an appointment of at least 60%. Large also needs a proven record as PI at Medium level. **A** (rank only). Sources: https://user.naiss.se/allocations/medium/, https://user.naiss.se/allocations/
4. Small allows at most 500 GPU-h/month on Arrhenius GPU, far below 20–30k GPU-h. Anything larger is Medium: monthly evaluation, submit by the 15th, start on the 1st. **Neither.** Sources: https://supr.naiss.se/round/2026small/, https://user.naiss.se/allocations/
5. Alvis cannot be applied for. It is decommissioned or retired, and its allocations move to Arrhenius. **A, B.** Sources: https://supr.naiss.se/round/2026small/, https://www.c3se.chalmers.se/
6. Berzelius (KAW, not NAISS):
   - The PI must be at least an assistant professor with a KAW affiliation, such as a KAW grant or WASP or another strategic-programme faculty post.
   - The project must sit within that affiliation, be AI/ML, be intended for publication, and use no sensitive data.

   **A, B.** Sources: https://www.nsc.liu.se/support/systems/berzelius-resource-allocation, https://supr.naiss.se/round/berzelius2026
7. NAISS terms of use say services may be used only for the purpose granted in the research plan, and research results should be published unless a commercial contract says otherwise. This blocks proprietary product training on NDA data even with an academic PI. **A** (B states publication only for Berzelius). Source: https://www.naiss.se/policies/terms-of-use/

**Next step: EuroHPC AI Factories Fast Lane Access (via Sweden AI Factory)**

8. The applicant must be an industry organisation (company, SME or startup) established in an EU Member State or associated country, and the PI must be affiliated with it. Access is free only to AI SMEs and startups for innovation purposes. **A, B.** Sources: Terms of Reference §2 (https://www.eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/document/download/bd7aa666-bdf3-4436-b5ec-0b34a781e817_en?filename=Terms%20of%20Reference%20-%20AIF%20Access%20Calls.pdf), https://eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/ai-factories/ai-factories-access-modes_en

10. The organisation's status must be proven with a valid VAT number, plus a PIC if applicable. **B.** Source: ToR §2
11. All e-mail addresses must be professional. Portal registration requires the organisation's official e-mail. **B.** Sources: ToR §4, https://access.eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/auth/register
12. Fast Lane is for HPC-ready users. Technical feasibility data is mandatory at submission, otherwise the proposal is administratively rejected, and it must pass the AI Factory's technical assessment. **A, B.** Source: ToR §1.2, §3.1, §5.1.1, §6d
13. Up to 50,000 GPU-h on a single system, granted first come, first served until the reserved resources run out. MareNostrum5 ACC and JUPITER Booster are currently unavailable. **A, B** (B does not name the unavailable systems). Sources: https://eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/fast-lane-access-ai-factories_en, ToR §5.1.1
14. The minimum request is 10,000 GPU-h per proposal (per-system table on the Fast Lane page). **Neither.** Source: https://eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/fast-lane-access-ai-factories_en (systems image)
15. Allocations last 1, 2 or 3 months, with no extensions and no additional resources. **A, B.** Sources: ToR §1.2, §4, https://swedenaifactory.se/about/faq/
16. A PI can in principle hold only one Fast Lane award at a time. A project that already has a running EuroHPC allocation is rejected as double-awarding. **A, B.** Source: ToR §1.2, §2
17. The project must have a civilian purpose and comply with the AI Act. The form includes eligibility and ethics self-assessments, and only English proposals are eligible. **B.** Source: ToR §2, §5.1.1
18. Allocations are for training and fine-tuning. Production use is not allowed, and inference only as a small, justified share. **B.** Source: EuroHPC Access Policy §1.6 (https://eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/document/download/30614065-6dd6-4206-8065-fc97948e5e2c_en)
19. The open call is the 2026 Fast Lane call, EHPC-AIF-2026FL (portal call 59), running 2026-01-21 to 2026-12-31.
    - The "Apply Now" button on the EuroHPC access-calls page links to the closed 2025 call (call 49, ended 2025-12-31).
    - No 2027 call is published.
    - In the last cycle no Fast Lane call was open from 1 to 21 January.

    **B.** Sources: https://access.eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/calls/59, https://api.access.eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/v2/calls/published, https://eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/ai-factories/ai-factories-access-calls/fast-lane-access-ai-factories_en
20. The applicant must sign the AI Factory's Acceptable Use Policy before getting access; onboarding is done by the AI Factory. **B.** Source: ToR §5.1
21. After the allocation:
    - A final report is due within 3 months and must include energy use and carbon footprint. EuroHPC may publish it after one year.
    - Results must be published except confidential parts, and EuroHPC and the AI Factory must be acknowledged.
    - Usage is monitored monthly.

## 3. Factual errors

**A has one error.**
- In turn 1, A says Alvis is replaced by "NAISS nya system Arrhenius, som också är akademiskt". It then says the founder will "troligen" run on "ett annat EuroHPC-system i Europa".
- Arrhenius is not academic only:
  - The SUPR EuroHPC Calls page says its European share is requested through EuroHPC calls.
  - The Fast Lane systems table lists **Arrhenius GPU (NAISS, SE)** with a 50,000 GPU-h maximum.
- So the founder can get Arrhenius in Linköping through Fast Lane, and A steers them away from it.
- This is an eligibility or availability claim, not an amount, date or open status, so I keep A2 at pass and apply the same rule to B.
- All other claims check out:
  - NAISS eligibility, Berzelius conditions, Alvis decommissioning.
  - Fast Lane: 4 working days, 1–3 months, single system, no extension, one award per PI, PI employment rule, unavailable MN5/JUPITER, complete technical part.
  - Playground: 5,000 GPU-h within 2 working days.
  - Sweden AI Factory: free; former Mimer; own system expected early 2027.
  - PDC's business-unit contact; Dardel ending on 2027-01-01 per SUPR.
  - The arithmetic: 25k GPU-h over 3 months is about 11–12 GPUs.

**B has none.**
- Every checkable claim matches an official page today:
  - NAISS (minimum doctoral student), Berzelius (KAW, publication), Alvis retired per C3SE, Small weekly and Berzelius monthly reviews.
  - Fast Lane free for AI SMEs, 4 working days, first come first served, 50k cap.
  - PI employment rule, VAT number, professional e-mail, English, ethics self-assessment, civilian purpose and AI Act.
  - No production use and only a small inference share, no double award, one award per PI, no extension.
  - Data and models stay with the company, AUP, final report with energy and CO2, publication after one year, SME exemption in the Access Policy.
  - The 2026 call open to 31 Dec 2026, the Apply Now link pointing to the closed 2025 call, and the reCAPTCHA on registration.
- B says the page lists neither which systems are open nor the cap per system. That is true of the access-calls variant B quoted, which shows no systems table. It is not an error.

## 5. Best recommendation: A

Both reach the same category: do not apply to NAISS, Berzelius or Alvis, and go to Sweden AI Factory and EuroHPC Fast Lane. What separates them is whether the advice survives the hidden profile.

- **A closes the research-partner route explicitly.** It says that even with a qualified academic PI, the results must be published and the machine may only be used for the stated purpose, which "passar dåligt" for training their own product model on customer data. It also flags the customer-contract question for moving NDA images, and gives a paid route (PDC business unit, EuroHPC commercial access) that fits the real goal of cutting GPU cost.
- **B leaves a dead end open.** It presents "en forskare vid t.ex. LiU söker som projektledare" as "det som ändrar det", without saying that NAISS use must be research for publication. That does not fit this founder.
- **The counterweight is A's January plan**, which relies on a call that is not yet published (section 4). B, by contrast, names the 31 December 2026 close date.
