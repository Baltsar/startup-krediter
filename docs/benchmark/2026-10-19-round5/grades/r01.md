## 1. Program

The program is Skatteverket's **Forskningsavdrag** ("Forskningsavdrag i arbetsgivardeklarationen"), the R&D reduction of employer contributions and allmän löneavgift under lag (2023:747). The name is unchanged. Two lagrådsremisser propose changes from 1 January 2027 but are not law. The June 2026 one would drop the 15-hour rule and widen the R&D definitions. The August 2026 one would add a voluntary 200 % extra deduction for R&D payroll costs in corporate income tax. Regeringen.se says whether they reach the 2027 budget depends on the election result. The budget proposition is due by 12 November 2026 and has not been submitted yet.

The hidden profile does not rule the founder out. Three of the four employees qualify (Sara, Ali, Mei), so there is no next-step program.

## 2. Stopper list

These are the requirements that would deny the deduction or get it rejected on audit:

1. **The work must meet the narrow legal R&D definition.** It must be systematic, qualified and done for a commercial purpose. Research must produce knowledge that is not already known. Development must use research results as a decisive prerequisite for a new or substantially improved product. Ordinary ongoing product development does not count. (Skatteverket forskningsavdrag page; Rättslig vägledning 1334)
2. **Each person must spend at least 50 % of actual working time on R&D in the calendar month.** (forskningsavdrag page; RV 1334)
3. **Each person must have at least 15 hours of actual working time in the calendar month.** (forskningsavdrag page)
4. **Vacation, sick leave and parental leave are not working time.** A month spent away counts only if the deduction was made under the main rule in each of the four preceding months. It never counts if the person did other tasks that month. (RV 1334)
5. **Only direct R&D work counts.** No deduction is allowed for staff in ledning och styrning, support, service, maintenance, quality assurance, marketing, administration, IT support or running operations. This excludes Johan, who spends about 20 % on R&D and the rest on sales, investors and admin. (forskningsavdrag page; RV 1334)
6. **The work must have a commercial purpose.** There is no deduction inside an R&D project run under public auspices and paid with public funds. (forskningsavdrag page; RV 1334)
7. **Age rules apply.** There is no deduction for anyone aged 67 or older at the start of the year. For people aged 18–22, the deduction is capped by the 10.21 % ålderspensionsavgift floor. (forskningsavdrag page)
8. **The employer must document its own case.** For each reporting period and each employee, it must be able to show every criterion and the extent of that person's R&D work. Examples are bemannings-PM, planning documents, time reports, project descriptions, how the R&D is financed and research reports. Skatteverket may ask for them. (forskningsavdrag page)
9. **Confidentiality agreements can block the evidence.** An NDA with a client can stop the company from handing documents to Skatteverket, and the deduction is then at risk. The documents may be delimited and anonymised. This is relevant here because of the district-heating data-sharing agreements. (forskningsavdrag page)
10. **There is no advance application.** The base goes in ruta 470 and the deduction in ruta 475 of the AGI, for every month in which an employee qualifies. (forskningsavdrag page)
11. **The base is paid remuneration that carries Swedish employer contributions.** No deduction can be made from egenavgifter, and with no salary there is nothing to deduct. (forskningsavdrag page; RV 1334)

**Count: A 11 of 11, B 9 of 11.**

A stated all eleven, mostly in turn 1, and applied 2, 5 and 8 to the founder's real numbers in turn 2.

B stated items 1–5, 7, 8, 10 and 11. It never mentioned the commercial-purpose and public-funds rule (6). It never mentioned confidentiality agreements (9), although it listed financing (own funds, grants, customer-paid) as a document to keep.

## 3. Factual errors

I found none in either conversation. I checked these figures against the official pages:
- 31.42 % falls to 11.42 %.
- 20 % of the base.
- The 3 MSEK monthly cap.
- The 67-year rule and the reduction for 18–22-year-olds.
- The four-month absence rule.
- The October AGI is due 12 November 2026 for turnover up to 40 MSEK.
- Corrections are possible up to six years back (B).
- The rule before July 2021 was 75 % (B).
- The 2027 proposals (B, and A in general terms).
- There is no proportioning: at 50 % or more, the whole salary counts (A).
- An unused deduction cannot be carried to a later month (A).
- A's arithmetic: 10,400 + 10,000 + 9,200 = 29,600 SEK a month, ruta 470 = 148,000 SEK, 355,200 SEK a year.
- B's illustration of about 480,000 SEK a year if all four qualify at 50,000 SEK.

Two minor imprecisions, which I did not count as errors:
- A says the 2027 proposals "avgörs i budgeten senast 12 november". Regeringen.se gives 12 November as the last day to submit the budget proposition, but the Riksdag decides later.
- A turn 1 says AGI is due "senast den 12:e månaden efter lönen". That is Skatteverket's own simplification ("de allra flesta"). In the 2026 table, July pay is due 17 August and November pay is due 14 December.

For the record, the hidden profile's "November AGI due 12 December 2026" is 14 December in Skatteverket's table. Neither assistant stated a November date.

## 5. Best recommendation

**A.** Both recommend claiming now in the AGI rather than applying anywhere. A's recommendation matches the hidden profile exactly:
- Johan's salary is out because he is under 50 % and in management.
- Sara, Ali and Mei are in.
- Ruta 470 is 148,000 SEK and ruta 475 is 29,600 SEK, starting with the October AGI due 12 November 2026.

B's founder never gave the percentages, so B could only give the rule. It flagged that "det är ofta vd:n som hamnar under 50 %" and advised including only those who clearly qualify, starting with November if October is hard to document.

B has one real advantage. Its turn 1 says, correctly, that past AGIs can be corrected six years back under the rules in force at the time. That matters a great deal here: this company has documented R&D since 2023 and never claimed, and its real goal is runway. A twice listed the same point as "fråga Skatteverket", although Skatteverket's own page answers it.

## 8. Verdict

**A.** A covers every stopper on the list and makes no factual errors. It answers the trap exactly: Johan out, 29,600 SEK a month for the other three. It also flags the next audit risks: Sara's management time as CTO, and Mei's pipeline operations versus model work. And it gives the founder working project-description and time-report documents.

A's real weakness is deferring retroactive correction to "fråga Skatteverket", which matters for this founder's runway goal. That does not outweigh its precision.
