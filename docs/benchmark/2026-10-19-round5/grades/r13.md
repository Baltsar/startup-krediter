## 1. Program

Innofounder from Danmarks Innovationsfond (the guidelines still say "Innovationsfonden"). The founder asked for "InnoFounder Graduate". That track no longer exists as its own track. The guidelines say Innofounder covers both recent graduates and people with substantial work or research experience, with a higher education as the common base. Both assistants corrected this premise. The founder is not ruled out of the programme: Ida and Rasmus meet every founder requirement on the facts given. So there is no next-step programme.

## 2. Stopper list

What the official pages say today: the programme page shows "Der er lige nu ingen søgemuligheder". The last timeline shown is e-grant open 28 July to 25 August 2026 at 12:00, pitch invitations mid-November 2026, panels December 2026 and final answers mid-January 2027. No 2027 round or deadline is published anywhere I could find. The trap is confirmed: guidelines section 1.2 requires every founder to have completed a public higher education, a PhD or an SU-eligible private higher education before the programme starts, as a rule of thumb at least 120 ECTS on one coherent programme. Jonas has 90 ECTS and no degree, so he cannot be a funded founder.

1. Education of at least 120 ECTS, completed before start, for every funded founder. Jonas is ruled out, so the most they can get is two founders, 55,000 DKK a month (guidelines 1.2; programme page). A: stated, but never applied to Jonas, because its founder never gave the education facts. B: stated and correctly applied.
2. Diploma or study documentation per applying founder attached at application; without it the application is rejected without assessment (guidelines 2, Bilag). A: yes, including the rejection consequence. B: yes.
3. At least 12.5% ownership per funded founder, documented before start (guidelines 1.2, 4.1). A: yes. B: yes.
4. Full time, at least 37 h a week, and at most 10 h a week of other education or paid work, week by week (guidelines 1.2; programme page). A: yes, including the week-by-week rule. B: yes.
5. Active participation and physical attendance in the sparring or incubation programme (guidelines 1.2). A: yes ("fysisk fremmøde"). B: yes (must take part in the incubation programme; Defence Tech accelerator for that theme).
6. Danish CVR number and NemKonto before start, not at application (guidelines 1.2, 4.2). A: yes. B: yes.
7. The company holds the rights to commercial exploitation of the idea (guidelines 1.2). A: yes, and it asked specifically about Ida's DTU job, DTU data and Jonas's freelance work. B: yes (put idea and code, including Jonas's, into the company).
8. The application must show who applies and who is the rest of the team; founders cannot be added or replaced after the deadline (guidelines 1.2). A: yes. B: yes, and it used this to explain that Jonas cannot join later.
9. Apply only through e-grant by a weekday 12:00 deadline, with no corrections afterwards. Formal non-compliance gives an administrative rejection. No call is open now: autumn 2026 closed 25 August 2026 and no next deadline is published (programme page; guidelines 2, 3). A: yes. B: yes.

A: yes (field names, the 1000-character limit, CV limit, milestone plan). B: yes (CV, 5+5 pages, PDF, 25 MB, three equally weighted criteria).
11. State-aid declarations at application: company and any controlling parent at most 5 years old, no distributed profit, not an undertaking in difficulty, at most 49 employees (guidelines 1.2, 6). A: yes. B: no; it mentioned only the 5-year age limit.
12. A company can be admitted only once (guidelines 1.2). A: yes. B: yes.
13. The idea is past the idea stage with significant development left, without substantial revenue or external capital, and not mainly about sales, marketing or scaling (guidelines 1.3; programme page). A: no. B: yes.

Total 13. A stated 12 and B stated 12. The counts are equal, but they are not equal in weight. B is the only one that turned item 1 into the decisive fact for this founder: Jonas cannot be funded. A never learned the education facts. Its turn-1 questions covered ownership, grants, full time and company, but not education, so A drafted with "Ansøgende founders (3)". A did list education as the first stopper to answer in turn 2.

A further point that matters for the draft: guidelines section 3 say every application is first checked against the formal requirements in section 1.2, and only applications that meet them are assessed. If Jonas is named as an applying founder, that risks more than his share: it puts the whole application through a formal check he fails. B's turn-1 line "Opfylder én af jer ikke kravene, får I kun tilskud for de andre" has no support in the guidelines, which do not say how an ineligible applying founder is handled. In practice B's turn-2 advice avoids the problem: apply with Ida and Rasmus and list Jonas as non-applying team.

## 3. Factual errors

A: none found. Every amount, date, contact and rule I checked matches the programme page or the autumn 2026 guidelines: 27,500 per founder; 82,500 for three; 100,000 development grant in total, 80% at start; monthly grant paid in arrears; 25 August 2026 at 12:00; 14 to 16 weeks to a final answer; start at the earliest two weeks after the signed grant letter; innofounder@innofond.dk; +45 61 90 50 00 on Thursdays 9 to 12; the 1000-character limit; theme "miljø, herunder vandmiljø"; the excluded development costs. A's SME and undertaking-in-difficulty lines (25% owned by public bodies, the investor exception, more than half the capital lost for a company over three years old) are on neither Innofounder page. They do match GBER Annex I Art. 3 and Art. 2(18)(a), which I fetched from EUR-Lex through the link in the guidelines.

B: one claim is contradicted. In turn 2 B says Jonas's pay "skal betales af andre penge". Guidelines section 1.4 say it is up to the company how much of the monthly grant is paid as salary and how much is used for other purposes in the company. B softens this with "spørg fonden, før I bruger noget af Ida og Rasmus' tilskud på ham". All of B's other figures check out: 55,000 a month and 660,000 over 12 months for two founders; 330,000 more for a third; spring 2026 open 20 January to 17 February 2026 with a single start date of 1 August 2026, confirmed by the fund's 2026 article and the spring guidelines; autumn 28 July to 25 August 2026; four-week windows; pitch answers within 9 weeks; the 5+5 page and 25 MB limits; the non-EU rules; the contacts.

## 5. Best recommendation: B

Given the hidden profile, the right advice is: no round is open; prepare for the next one; apply with Ida and Rasmus as the funded founders and Jonas as a non-applying team member, unless he finishes a qualifying degree before start; get the CVR before start; check the start date against their mid-2027 plan with the fund. B gives exactly this, with the correct two-founder amounts. It also explains the cost of waiting for Jonas: a missed round, and a company can be admitted only once. A's advice to prepare for the next round is right, and its stopper coverage is excellent. But it is still heading toward a three-founder application and has not found the decisive problem.

## 8. Verdict: B

Both answers are accurate and cover almost the same stoppers. B asked for each founder's education in its first reply. That let it catch the trap: Jonas, with 90 ECTS and no degree, cannot be a funded founder. B turned this into a concrete plan: apply for Ida and Rasmus at 55,000 DKK a month, and keep Jonas as team.
