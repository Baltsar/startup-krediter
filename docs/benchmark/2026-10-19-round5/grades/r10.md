## 1. Program

The program is Oppstartstilskudd 1 from Innovasjon Norge. It is the early stage of the old etablerertilskudd, so the founder's premise about the name is right.

- Applications are rolling.
- The grant is at most 150,000 NOK.
- Processing currently takes 3-4 weeks.

There is no separate next-step program. The founder is shut out only because her company is an ENK, and the fix is to found an AS and apply from it. That is a company-law step, not a program, so `next_step_program` is null. For founders who have not finished the idea phase, the page points to Innovasjon Norge's incubators and næringshager, and to Raskavklaring, a guidance form rather than a program.

## 3. Factual errors

Neither conversation states anything on the program that an official page contradicts.

**A.** All program figures and rules check out:
- 150,000 NOK and 100% of invoiced costs
- the 50/50 payout
- the 9+3 month project period
- 3-4 weeks processing
- under 5 years
- the ownership thresholds
- the part-time wording
- the "frittstående apper" wording

A's AS facts also check out:
- share capital of at least 30,000 NOK, confirmed by a bank, auditor, lawyer or authorised accountant (Altinn)
- a 6,825 NOK digital fee for 2026 (Altinn and the Brreg fee page)
- registration through Samordnet registermelding

A's Brreg-role claim is supported by the Kundesenter page, which covers Nettbank and payout.

**B.** All program figures and rules check out:
- 150,000 NOK and 100% of invoiced costs
- 50% pre-financing
- 3-4 weeks
- "under fem år, normalt under tre", which matches the guide article
- Raskavklaring taking 15-20 minutes with an answer in 1-2 days

B's AS facts check out too: 30,000 NOK share capital, a 6,825 NOK fee for 2026, and registering beneficial owners within 14 days. B also says founding through a bank or accounting software is faster. Brreg's six partner providers are DNB, Fiken, Firmafabrikk, Folio, SpareBank 1 and Tripletex, which supports that.

B checked several things I could confirm independently:
- **The name:** "Tidvis AS" (2020, game software), Tidvis Formidling AS, Tidvis Games AS and Tidvis Utvikling AS all exist in Enhetsregisteret.
- **The SSB counts:**
  - Food service businesses with 5-49 employees in 2026: 97+129+75 = 301 in Trondheim and 2,510+2,259+1,013 = 5,782 in Norway.
  - Employment in "Drift av restauranter og kafeer" in 2025: 54,260.
- **Arbeidsmiljøloven § 10-3** (drøftes med tillitsvalgte senest to uker før) is stated correctly in turn 2.

Minor imprecisions, not counted as errors:
- B's turn-1 draft paraphrased § 10-3 as the rota having to be "kjent for de ansatte" two weeks ahead. B corrected this in turn 2.
- B says registration "tar inntil 10 virkedager", or 5 days digitally. Altinn says the case is *taken up for processing* within 10 days, or 5 days with electronic founding.

## 4. Untraceable figures

- "Ingen pågående betalingsanmerkninger, verken for deg eller selskapet" (turn 1). The page speaks only of businesses. A corrected this itself in turn 2.

**B:** none.

## 5. Best recommendation: B

Both reach the right conclusion: the ENK cannot apply. Found an AS, do the idea-phase customer work, then apply. A also raises the "overtakelse av eksisterende virksomhet" question, which is a fair caution.

The tiebreaker is B's plan:
- found the AS and re-interview the 9 restaurants in November
- apply at the end of November
- get an answer in December
- start the project in January 2027

That fits her hidden deadline of a decision by January 2027. B also makes the customer findings a precondition before sending.

A handles the full-time question more carefully. It asks rather than assumes, which suits her goal of testing before quitting. But A gives no timeline.

## 6. Draft: B

The founder asked twice for help writing the application. A wrote a competitor-analysis draft and declined to draft the application until an AS exists, so it gave her nothing to fill in.

B drafted for the future AS ("[navn] AS") and said plainly that she cannot send yet. Since her fit is conditional and founding an AS fixes it, this is not drafting for a program she is ruled out of.

What B's draft does well:
- It follows the page's sections in order, with hypotheses, activities and goals inserted after Løsning og usikkerhet.
- It marks gaps in brackets.
- It applies the rules: own time out, VAT out, the pilot cost removed until a real invoice exists.
- The arithmetic is correct:
  - 85,000 NOK applied for
  - 42,500 NOK at start
  - 106,250 NOK including VAT
  - about 63,750 NOK of the AS's own funds needed during the project
  - 21,250 NOK of VAT as her own cost
- It handles the personal data in the pilots: data processing agreement, data minimisation and the AI Act question. Her hidden profile flags this.

B's flaw: the draft states three things as fact, unbracketed, that the founder never said:
- the five pilot restaurants "har bekreftet det på e-post (vedlegg)"
- "Fra januar 2027 jobber jeg fullt i [navn] AS"
- "Jeg mangler kompetanse til å bygge selve KI-løsningen"
