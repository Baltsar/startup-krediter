## 1. Program

The program is **Starttiraha**. The local employment authority grants it under law 380/2023, 99–101 §. In Helsinki that authority is Helsingin työllisyyspalvelut. The founder called it "TE-toimiston starttiraha".

The TE offices were abolished on 1.1.2025, and the employment areas took over. The program has not been renamed or replaced. Since 1.5.2026 the amount is tied to the earnings-related allowance's basic part, which equals Kela's yleistuki: 37,21 €/day in 2026.

## The trap, checked

The trap holds. Law 380/2023, 100 § 2 mom 3 kohta, says starttiraha is not granted if the applicant started full-time business, or expanded a side business to full time, before the application reached the employment authority. The only exception is a continuation period within the maximum duration.

Several official pages say the same:
- the Työmarkkinatori service page
- the Työmarkkinatori news item from 3.7.2026
- the Helsinki page ("Jos toimit jo päätoimisena yrittäjänä ennen kuin starttirahahakemus on saapunut Helsingin työllisyyspalveluille")
- the official form's own preamble

Laura has worked full time in Sanaseppä Oy since 1.9.2026. The Oy was registered on 4.9.2026, and she sent invoices on 30.9 and 15.10. She never had starttiraha, so the continuation exception cannot apply. She was in salaried work before, not running a side business, so the side-business expansion route is closed too. **She is ruled out.**

The trap's second half also holds. The application goes to the employment area, here the City of Helsinki, through Työmarkkinatori. It does not go to a TE office.

- 6 months at a time, 12 months at most
- payment applied for in arrears within two months

- the Helsinki attachment list
- Helsinki's advice to apply about four weeks ahead
- the trap rule and the Business Helsinki booking URL

One imprecision: A's turn-1 list of income deducted from the grant includes "etuudet" without the exception. Both the form and hel.fi exempt "etuoikeutetut tulot" such as child benefit, housing allowance and toimeentulotuki. This affects the amount, not eligibility, so I do not count it as an error.

**B: one error.** In T1 B says VAT registration is mandatory if turnover exceeds 20 000 € "tilikaudessa". Vero.fi measures the threshold over the calendar year, "even if the financial year exceeds 12 months". The 20 000 € figure itself is right. B gives the correct calendar-year rule in T2, and the point is outside the program.

These B claims all checked out:

- the 6+6 months and the TE reform
- Helsinki's CV requirement
