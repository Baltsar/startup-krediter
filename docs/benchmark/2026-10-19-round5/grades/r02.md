## 1. Program

Innovativa Startups 2026 (Vinnova, dnr 2025-04831). It is open: it opened 12 Oct 2026 and closes 12 Jan 2027 at 14:00. Decision is on 23 Mar 2027. Per the Swedish page, the project may start on 23 Mar 2027 and must start by 30 Mar 2027 at the latest. It may run for at most nine months. The grant is at most SEK 500,000, at up to 100 % of costs. The program has not been renamed or replaced. The hidden profile fits every formal requirement, so there is no next-step program.

The trap's "1 April at the latest" comes from the machine-translated English page. That page has contradictory date tiles (24 Mar, 30 Mar and 1 Apr) and also says 9 Nov for the second information meeting. The Swedish page says 23 Mar to 30 Mar and 2 and 6 Nov. The English page itself says the Swedish text applies.

## 3. Factual errors

I found no claim in either conversation that an official page contradicts.

Checked and correct in both:
- dates (opening, deadline 14:00, decision, start window 23-30 Mar, nine months)
- the SEK 500,000 cap at up to 100 %
- the GBER art. 22 versus de minimis split at 5 years, and the EUR 300,000 over three years
- indirect costs at 30 %
- the employer registration rule
- the share-capital rule as worded by Vinnova
- the payroll-tax arithmetic (31.42 % matches Vinnova's own worked example)

Checked and correct in A:
- information meetings on 2 and 6 Nov, and that the English page says 9 Nov
- the 20 % consultant cap in the 2025 call, confirmed on the 2025 call text; the 2026 text has no cap but requires justification
- Upphandlingsmyndigheten's 2024 figures: 17,575 procurements, SEK 931bn, 4.6 bids, SMEs about 75 % of bids, all confirmed in the agency's press release of 5 Feb 2026

Checked and correct in B:
- payout follows a preliminary plan, with the first payment after the start report (allmänna villkor 2026 §6.2)
- payout only to bankgiro or plusgiro
- AI help is allowed and the applicant answers for the content
- whole SEK and year columns in the budget
- B's own character counts are accurate: 1,251, 126 and 1,354, re-counted
- B's ownership thresholds match the EU SME definition guide on vinnova.se: 25 % for another company or a public body, and up to 50 % for venture funds, business angels or universities

Both budgets add up:
- **A:** 283,867 + 179,783 + 198,707 = 662,357 in salaries; total 867,357; 57.6 % grant.
- **B:** option A 484,137 at 100 %; option B 763,274 at 65.5 %.

One nuance applies to both. Both said that under the EU "undertaking in difficulty" definition (GBER art. 2(18)(a)), share premium counts as share capital and the under-three-years exception no longer applies. That is a correct reading of art. 2(18). GBER art. 1(4)(c) also exempts start-up aid schemes (art. 22) from GBER's own exclusion of undertakings in difficulty, which neither mentioned. That exemption does not settle Vinnova's separate pre-decision check, though, if Vinnova reads its rule with the EU definition. Vinnova does not say how it counts, so neither statement is contradicted. Both told the founder to ask Vinnova before writing more and put this point at the top. The question writer counts this founder as passing the rule, so for her it is most likely a non-issue.

A's line that formal questions "besvaras bara via startups@vinnova.se" overstates the page, which simply lists that address for questions. It is not an amount or a date.

## 5. Best recommendation

The founder fits. The right advice is to apply this round, before Christmas, with the Nordiq line removed or replaced by work inside the project period.

**Tie.** Both conversations land on the same recommendation: apply now, after checking the share-capital point with Vinnova. Neither one's final recommendation removes Nordiq:
- **A** never raises the timing. If the founder follows A, she submits SEK 150,000 of consultancy that is already ordered for Nov 2026-Jan 2027. That cost is ineligible. At A's stödnivå of about 57.6 %, losing it cuts the payable grant to about SEK 413,000.
- **B** gave the rule in turn 1, but its turn-2 budget options and Finansieringsplan still contain the line.

B's edge is knowing the stopper, and that is scored in item 18 and the verdict. It is not counted again here.

## 6. Draft

The founder asked for the project description and the "why we need funding" text, then for a finished version.

**A.** A's draft is more useful to submit as content. It addresses every official criterion with founder facts:
- **Potential:** problem, solution, competitor gap left as a placeholder, verified market size, international potential via EU directives and TED, knowledge assets.
- **Genomförbarhet:** work packages, risks, a full budget with correct arithmetic and the 100 % rate, the 30 % overhead handled, a cash gap to September-December flagged, the revenue inconsistency (6 × at most 4,000 × 12 = 288,000 against 420,000) flagged, and consultant motivation.
- **Team:** roles, the 78 % founder control, a placeholder for the gender-equality strategy, and the gender-perspective form question.

A also gives Swedish and English summaries. It correctly declines to present customer tenders under a data processing agreement as a training asset until the contracts allow it. Gaps are marked, and its time-share percentages are labelled as its own assumptions for the founder to set.

A's weaknesses:
- It cannot match the form's field limits; it says so and asks for them.
- It carries the ineligible Nordiq line.
- It states one product capability the founder never gave: the flags come "med hänvisning till var i underlaget de står". This fails A9.

**B.** B's texts are shaped for the form and short. But they cover only three fields: the summary, the goal and the financing plan. Problem, solution, competitiveness, work packages and team are deferred to a later turn. The field limits B builds on cannot be verified. B's summary states that the model is trained on customers' own tenders under the data processing agreement, which A rightly flagged as a legal risk. B also keeps Nordiq in the texts despite its own turn-1 warning. B's two budget options are clear and correct.

**Verdict on the draft: A.**

## 8. Verdict

**B, narrowly.**
