## 1. Program

**Innofounder**, run by Danmarks Innovationsfond. The site now uses that name. The guidelines still say "Innovationsfonden". The "Graduate" track the founder asked about no longer exists. Innofounder is now one 12-month programme for recent graduates and for people with substantial work or research experience (guidelines §1.1). It is not renamed or replaced in a way that needs a next-step program. The hidden profile does not rule the team out: Ida and Rasmus are eligible. Only Jonas cannot be a funded founder. So `next_step_program` is null.

## 2. Official facts that decide this case

- **Status.** Program page: "Der er lige nu ingen søgemuligheder". The last timeline was e-grant open 28 July to 25 August 2026 at 12:00, rejection or pitch invitation mid-November 2026, panel days in December 2026, final answer mid-January 2027. No 2027 deadline is published.
- **2026 pattern.** Per the 12 Dec 2025 article: spring 2026 was open 20 Jan to 17 Feb 2026, with a fixed programme start on 1 August.
- **Money.** DKK 27,500 per founder per month for 12 months. Three founders get 82,500 a month and two get 55,000. There is one development grant of DKK 100,000 for the team, paid 80% at start and 20% after the approved final accounts. It cannot pay salaries, rent, office PCs or audit.
- **Education (the trap).** By programme start, each founder must have completed a public ordinary higher education, a PhD, or an SU-eligible private higher education. The rule of thumb is at least 120 ECTS on one coherent programme (guidelines §1.2.3, and the program page says "mindst 120 ECTS-point"). Jonas has 90 ECTS and no degree, so he cannot be a funded founder. The guidelines name no route in through work experience. The programme "forudsætter et videregående uddannelsesniveau som fælles fagligt afsæt" (§1.1). The guidelines do allow team members who do not meet the requirements, but the application must say clearly who applies and who makes up the rest of the team (§1.2.2). The trap holds when checked against the official pages: only Ida (300 ECTS) and Rasmus (180 ECTS) can receive the 27,500 a month.

## 4. Factual errors

**A:** none found. Every amount, date, status and contact matches the program page or the guidelines: 27,500 per founder; 82,500 for three; DKK 100,000 for the whole team at 80/20; closed with the last deadline 25 Aug 2026; 14-16 weeks to a final answer; the last round ending mid-January 2027; start at the earliest two weeks after the signed grant letter; innofounder@innofond.dk and +45 61 90 50 00, Thursdays 9-12.

Minor points that are not errors:

- "MitID eller eget brugernavn": the guidelines still say NemID.
- The SME thresholds ("25 %, fonde og angels: over 50 %") and the firm-in-difficulty rule ("over tre år og halvdelen af kapitalen tabt") come from GBER, not from the Innofounder pages, but GBER art. 2(18) and Annex I are what guidelines §6 refers to.

**B:** none found. The spring 2026 window of 20 Jan to 17 Feb with a 1 August start checks out against the 2026 article, and so does "plan published mid-December" (the article is dated 12 Dec 2025). These also check out: four weeks of e-grant per round; autumn 28 Jul to 25 Aug 2026 at 12:00; answer within 9 weeks; 55,000 a month and 660,000 in total for two; 330,000 more for a third; the 5+5 pages and 25 MB limits; the contact details. "Danmarks Innovationsfond (det tidligere Innovationsfonden)" matches the current site branding.

One soft overstatement in B: "hans løn skal betales af andre penge". Guidelines §1.5 let the company decide how much of the monthly grant goes to salary and how much to other purposes. B hedges this at once with "Spørg fonden", so I did not count it as an error.
