## Assistant, turn 1
Sweden has no national grant just for game studios; general innovation grants can fund your AI work. Free GPU time is your strongest route, assuming a Swedish company.

Compute: ● Sweden AI Factory, free help picking the EuroHPC call and checking the application; ◐ EuroHPC Playground, 5 000 GPU hours free for 1 to 3 months.
Cloud credits: ◐ AWS Activate Founders, 1 000 USD, up to 5 000 USD; ◐ Google for Startups Cloud, Start tier 2 000 USD.
Grants: ◐ Vinnova Innovativa Startups 2026, up to 500 000 SEK, closes 12 January 2027; ◐ Almi Verifieringsmedel, often up to 100 000 SEK, outside services only.

GPU hours cover training; credits can also run the models in your game; grants pay project costs.

Before the details:
1. Is the company an aktiebolag (AB) or a sole trader? Most of these depend on it (Vinnova, Almi and 3 more).
2. Are any of you at a university as student, researcher or staff? Free startup support; IP rules may apply.
3. Has the company lost more than half its share capital? Vinnova excludes companies that have.

Answer the questions, or pick a route, and I'll go through what each application requires.
