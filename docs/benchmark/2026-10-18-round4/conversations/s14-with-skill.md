# s14 with-skill

## Founder, turn 1
Hi! We're 3 people building an AI tool that separates stems (vocals/drums/bass) from live DJ sets in real time, so DJs can remix on the fly. We need roughly €40k worth of GPU compute over the next 6 months to train the model, plus ideally some grant money so we can work on it full time for 6 months instead of evenings. What programs should we be applying to?

## Assistant, turn 1
GPU time and grant money usually come from different programs. Public compute programs give you machine hours, not cash. A grant is what can pay you to work on this full time. So you'll probably want one of each, and both depend on where you are.

Before I map the options:

1. Which country is the company registered in (or will be), and where do you live? Every grant and GPU program here is tied to a country.
2. Is there a company yet: a limited company, a sole trader, or not yet? Most of these programs depend on it.
3. Are any of you a student, researcher or university employee right now? Universities run free startup support, and their IP rules may apply.

Answer these and I'll lay out the realistic routes for GPU time and for funding, and what decides each one. Since this is music tech, I'll also check creative-industry funding in your country.

## Founder, turn 2
1. Norway. We'll register the company as an AS in Oslo, and all three of us live in Oslo.
2. Not yet, we're planning to register an AS.
3. No, none of us is at a university.

## Assistant, turn 2
Free GPU time fits what you've told me. Grant money for your own salaries is harder. Startup grant 1 pays only for external services, and Startup grant 2 covers at most half, matched by your own money.

Everything below needs the AS registered first.

Compute: ● Sigma2 KI-fabrikken, Norway's AI Factory, no price or hours published; ◐ EuroHPC AI Factories Playground, free GPU hours for 1 to 3 months (Fast Lane: up to 50 000).

Grants: ◐ Innovasjon Norge Startup grant 1, up to NOK 150 000; ◐ Startup grant 2, up to NOK 1 000 000 (2026 budget cut by 60 %).

Tax relief: ● SkatteFUNN, 19 % back on approved R&D costs you've paid, in cash if the AS pays no tax.

Before the details:
1. Will you work full time in the AS, or keep day jobs? Innovasjon Norge rarely backs part-time ventures.
2. Will the project lead stay employed by the AS three months past the GPU period? EuroHPC asks for it.
3. Have you run your training code on an HPC cluster before? Fast Lane is for HPC-familiar teams.

Answer these, or pick a route, and I'll go through what applying requires.
