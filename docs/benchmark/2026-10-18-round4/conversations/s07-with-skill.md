Sweden AI Factory is a free, public support service run by NAISS and RISE. It helps you get free time on EU supercomputers, has gaming among its five expert focus areas and takes no share of your IP, so it fits you well.

**Before you apply**
- Contact form: name, email, company, sector (SME/Startup), focus area (there is a Gaming option), the expertise you need (Compute resources, GDPR and others), and a message about the project and your compute need.
- Choosing the call: Playground gives 5 000 GPU hours for 1, 2 or 3 months, with access within 2 working days, for new industry users. Fast Lane gives 50 000 GPU hours for 3 months, with access within 4 working days, for teams used to running AI on HPC systems.
- The GPU application itself is yours, in the EuroHPC portal. They advise and review it; you submit.
- Hands-on expert help, in some cases for up to a month (fine-tuning, open-source models). You have to match their technical effort and bring the domain knowledge.
- Training events are free but need a company email; Gmail, Outlook or Yahoo are not accepted.
- The supercomputers are for development and training. Running the models in your live game needs a cloud provider; that is where AWS or Google credits fit.
- Allocations cannot be extended; when the hours run out, you apply again.
- If the work ends up in a publication or presentation, acknowledge Sweden AI Factory with the sentence in its FAQ.

**Unclear**
- What the message should contain beyond "tell us about your AI project". Ask info@swedenaifactory.se.
- Response time: the form only says "shortly". Ask info@swedenaifactory.se.
- Publishing results: the FAQ says startups and SMEs need not publish. EuroHPC's Playground terms (March 2026 version) ask for a final report and published results, except confidential parts. Ask aif.access@eurohpc-ju.europa.eu or info@swedenaifactory.se before training on anything confidential.
- Its compute page shows two versions with different figures for the larger calls (AI for Science 90 000 or 360 000 GPU hours, Large Scale 22 or 30 million). Check the EuroHPC call page before planning on them.
- Data protection: each supercomputer has its own measures, and the FAQ leaves it to you to judge whether they are enough. If you train on recordings of real voice actors, that is personal data; choose GDPR on the form and ask before uploading any.
- Its own supercomputer is expected early 2027, and its funding is secured until March 2028. Ask what happens after that if you plan long term.
