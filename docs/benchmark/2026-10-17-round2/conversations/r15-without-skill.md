## Assistant, turn 1
Short answer: don't register in Germany before you move. Wait and set up a Swedish company once you're in Stockholm, and only when there's something concrete that needs one. Here's why.

**1. The AI/cloud credits don't care much which country you're in.**
OpenAI, Anthropic and AWS don't give you more credits for being a German or a Swedish company. What changes the amount is who backs you.
- **AWS Activate Founders** is self-serve and open in most countries, but it's small. Most sources say about $1,000. The large AWS amounts come through an "Activate Provider", meaning an accelerator, incubator or VC.
- **Anthropic** has a public application with small amounts. Recent reports say around $1,000 in API credits. The bigger tiers, up to about $100k, go to startups backed by one of their partner VCs. Anthropic's stated terms also mention institutional equity funding and a company founded within the last 4 years, so a solo bootstrapped prototype may not get in yet. The amounts and conditions change often, so check their own page before planning around a number.
- **OpenAI** works much the same way. The meaningful credits mostly come through partners and accelerators.

So for credits, the thing that matters is getting into a program, not your country of registration. In Stockholm that could be SUP46, STING, Norrsken, Antler or KTH Innovation. That last one may be relevant since your partner is at KTH, though check whether they'd take a non-KTH founder. Most of these programs want a local company, which is another reason to register in Sweden.

**2. The Swedish grant needs a Swedish aktiebolag.**
The main one for your stage is Vinnova's **Innovativa startups**, with grants up to SEK 500k. It requires a Swedish AB with fewer than 10 employees and at most SEK 10M in turnover or balance sheet. A German GmbH or UG can't apply. The current round is open from 12 Oct 2026 to 12 Jan 2027. You'd need an AB registered and a strong application in by early January, which is very tight if you're only arriving that month. I'd plan for the next round rather than rush. Almi is the other early-stage option, mostly loans and some verification funding, and it also wants a Swedish company.

**3. A German company you run from Sweden causes problems.**
If you set up a UG or GmbH now and then run it from Stockholm, both countries may claim to tax it. Germany would look at the registered seat and Sweden at where it's actually run. You'd also have German bookkeeping and Steuerberater costs, plus a company that can't get Swedish grants. Moving it or closing it later is slow and expensive. For a solo prototype with no revenue, that's all cost and no benefit.

**What I'd do:**
- Keep building now without a company. Apply for anything that doesn't need incorporation, like AWS Founders on a personal account and hackathon or provider perks.
- Once you're in Stockholm with a personnummer, register an AB. It needs SEK 25,000 in share capital, which you can use for the business.
- Time the AB to the first thing that actually requires it: an accelerator, the next Vinnova round, or your first paying customer.

A few things would change the advice, so it would help to know:
1. Are you an EU/EEA citizen? If not, your right to work and run a business in Sweden comes through your partner's permit, and the details matter.
2. Do you have paying customers in Germany already, or expect revenue before January?
3. Are you hoping to raise money from investors soon, or stay bootstrapped for now?
4. Do you need money in the next few months, or is this more about planning for 2027?

Quick caveat: I'm not a tax advisor. Before you register anything, a 30-minute call with a Swedish accountant (or Verksamt.se, the government's free startup guide) is worth it, mainly to get the move right on your personal tax.

Sources: [Vinnova – Innovative startups 2026](https://www.vinnova.se/en/calls-for-proposals/innovative-startups/innovative-startups-2026), [Lund University – info on the 2026 call](https://www.ch.lu.se/evenemang/info-meeting-vinnovas-call-innovative-startups-2026), [AWS – applying for Activate credits](https://aws.amazon.com/aws-startups/learn/applying-for-aws-activate-credits-a-step-by-step-guide/), [Anthropic startup credits guide (third-party)](https://guptadeepak.com/anthropic-claude-for-startups-the-complete-guide-to-credits-tiers-and-eligibility-2026/), [Report on Anthropic's startup program, Oct 2026](https://androidheadlines.com/2026/10/anthropic-expands-claude-startups-program.html)

## Assistant, turn 2
Thanks, that makes it simpler.

**Don't set up a company yet, in either country.** You have no revenue, no customers and no investors. A company now would only bring bookkeeping, annual reports and fees, and you'd get nothing from it. Skip Germany completely.

**Being an EU citizen means the move is easy.** You can live, work and run a business in Sweden without a permit. If you're staying a year or more, register with Skatteverket when you arrive to get a personnummer. Most other things depend on it, like the bank, BankID and registering a company. It can take a few weeks, so do it in your first week.

**For "not sure" on funding, the plan is:**

*Now until January (no company needed):*
- Keep building the prototype. That's what any program will judge you on.
- Apply for the small credits you can get without a company, like AWS Activate Founders.
- Keep API spend low with the cheapest models that do the job. Credits won't stretch far for video and transcription work.

*January to spring, once you're in Stockholm:*
- Get your personnummer.
- Talk to people before you register anything. KTH Innovation, STING and SUP46 run free intro meetings. Ask them whether they'd take you, and what they'd want to see first.
- Get 5–10 podcasters actually using your clips. That shows you whether this is a company, and every grant or investor asks for it.

*Register a Swedish AB when one of these happens:*
- an accelerator accepts you (that also unlocks the bigger AWS, OpenAI and Anthropic credits),
- you're ready for the next Vinnova Innovativa startups round (up to SEK 500k, AB required),
- someone wants to pay you or invest.

It costs SEK 25,000 in share capital plus a registration fee. You can spend the capital on the business afterwards. It's usually done in a week or two on Verksamt.se.

**One thing to be honest about:** the current Vinnova round closes 12 January 2027. Getting a personnummer, an AB and a good application ready by then is very unlikely, so aim for the next round. Vinnova also wants to see that you've tested customer need. A prototype plus real users will make that application much stronger.
