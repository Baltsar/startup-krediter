## Founder, turn 1
Hi, we're a seed-funded HR tech startup (Delaware C-corp, team in SF) and my cofounder is Swedish. We'd love to tap into Vinnova's Innovative Startups grant to fund our European expansion. Can we apply since he's a Swedish citizen? We don't have a Swedish entity yet but could set one up if needed.

## Assistant, turn 1
Citizenship doesn't matter here. Vinnova looks at the company, not the founders. Your Delaware C-corp can't apply. A new Swedish AB that does business in Sweden could. But as a subsidiary of your US company, the size limits apply to the whole group. That means 10 or fewer full-time equivalents in total, and net revenue or balance sheet of 10 MSEK or less (one of the two may be higher, not both). There's a second catch. The grant pays for a project of up to nine months that tests the market potential of an innovative idea. It doesn't pay for an expansion.

It's a grant of up to 500 000 SEK for a project of up to nine months that tests whether an innovative idea has a market. You can't use it to fund an expansion, and right now your headcount rules you out.

**What the application asks for, in case your situation changes:**
- An account in Vinnova's application portal (ansok.vinnova.se)
- An application in English or Swedish. One per company per round.
- A named signatory and a project leader for the Swedish company
- Answers on three criteria: potential, feasibility (budget, plan to sell, plan to finance, why you need Vinnova's money) and team (the founders control the company, plus a plan for gender balance in who has influence)
- A full project budget in whole SEK. Vinnova covers up to 100% of costs, at most 500 000 SEK.
- The company must not have bought another business or been formed by a merger.
- If the grant counts as de minimis aid (one of the EU's state aid rules), you sign a form declaring earlier aid. The cap is 300 000 EUR over three years, and aid to your whole group counts toward it.
- A signed approval form from each project party
- Vinnova may audit the project up to ten years after the last payment.
- Mark the application complete before 12 January 2027 at 14:00. An application you haven't marked complete is not registered.

**Unclear on the pages:**
- Whether the 10 full-time equivalents are counted over the last financial year or at the time you apply. Ask startups@vinnova.se.
- Whether founders "control the company" when a US parent owns it. Ask startups@vinnova.se.
- How Vinnova picks who goes into the lottery. Experts score every application, and among applications rated equally strong, Vinnova draws lots. Ask startups@vinnova.se.
- How you log in to the portal (BankID or password). Ask helpdesk@vinnova.se.

Link: https://www.vinnova.se/e/innovativa-startups/innovativa-startups-2026
