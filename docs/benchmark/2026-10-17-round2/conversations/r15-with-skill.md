## Assistant, turn 1
On funding, wait and register in Sweden. Credits programs don't care where the company is registered. The Swedish money needs a Swedish company, or you living in Sweden, and a German GmbH would open none of it. Tax and legal setup are a separate question that I'm not covering here.

Will you have a Swedish personnummer and a Swedish bank account once you've moved?

● Almi Verifieringsmedel: grant of up to 25 000 SEK to you as a private person, or often up to 100 000 SEK to a company. What decides it: a personnummer, a Swedish bank account and a plan to start the company in Sweden. 6 requirements in the application, 2 unclear points.
◐ AWS Activate Founders: 1 000 USD in credits to start, up to 5 000 USD, from any country. What decides it: you need a company, a paid AWS account and a website. 9 requirements, 3 unclear points.
× Claude Startups: closed right now because the cohort is full, and no reopening date is published. OpenAI gives credits only through partner VCs.

Which one do you want to look at? Then you get everything the application asks for.

A Swedish AB would also open Vinnova Innovativa Startups (grant of up to 500 000 SEK, closes 12 January 2027 at 14:00), Almi Innovationslån, Almi Tillväxtlån, NVIDIA Inception and 10 more.

## Assistant, turn 2
```
●  Almi Verifieringsmedel (Verification Grant)
●  in Sweden
●  a company, a sole trader or no company yet
→  Companies in many cases up to 100 000 SEK; private individuals up to 25 000 SEK; amount set with the advisor and varies by region
```
Almi Verifieringsmedel is a grant, not a loan. It pays for outside help to test whether your idea holds up, and you normally don't have to match it with your own money. It fits you once you're settled in Stockholm, and only if you can get a personnummer and a Swedish bank account. A German GmbH wouldn't count, because Almi aims it at Swedish small companies.

**Before you apply**
- You can't apply directly. You first talk to an Almi advisor, who then gives you access to Almi's online application.
- Applying as a private person (before you have a company), you need all three: a Swedish personnummer, an account with a Swedish bank, and a clear plan to start the company in Sweden. Without any one of them, Almi won't grant it.
- The money only buys outside services that test the idea. Almi's examples are checking customer needs and market potential, market and competitor analysis, novelty or freedom-to-operate checks, legal or regulatory reviews, and building a simple MVP.
- It can't pay for travel, entertainment, marketing, training, patent applications, or prototypes and investments that keep their value.
- Almi judges whether the idea is innovative and has room to grow. You set the amount with your advisor, and it varies by region.
- Be ready to show a short description of the idea and business model, what you want to test and why, how you'll test it, and a simple budget and timeline.
- It counts as small-scale public aid ("de minimis"). Your total of this kind of aid can't go over 300 000 EUR across the current year and the three before it, and all earlier public aid counts.
- Work paid by the grant can only start after approval. Almi says processing usually takes up to a few weeks once your application is complete.

**Unclear**
- Whether Almi Stockholm pays the full 25 000 SEK to a private person, or up to 100 000 SEK to a company. The page says it depends on the region. Ask your advisor.
- Whether it can pay for your own work. The page only mentions outside services. Ask before you plan the budget.
- It lists "a simple MVP" as allowed but "prototypes" as not allowed, so it's unclear where building your clipping tool falls. Ask the advisor before you budget for development.

**Next step:** Once you've moved, book a meeting with an Almi advisor about the idea and what you need to test. The advisor then opens the online application, and you submit it yourself. https://www.almi.se/lan-finansiering/verifieringsmedel/

On the personnummer: whether you get one depends on your move, which Skatteverket (the Swedish Tax Agency) handles. Until you have one, this door stays shut to you as a private person. Registering an AB opens the company route, which is often up to 100 000 SEK.
