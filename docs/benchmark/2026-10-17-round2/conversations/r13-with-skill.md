## Founder, turn 1
Founder here, we're an AS registered in Trondheim but half our team (3 people) sits in Linköping. We build counter-drone detection software (passive RF + vision), dual-use but mainly defence customers. Raised NOK 12M pre-seed last year. Need GPU for training on ~40TB of sensor data and want to know what Nordic/EU defence money we can go after. Our swedish devs are contractors via their own firmor if that matters

## Assistant, turn 1
For GPU time, Norway's AI Factory at Sigma2 looks like the best fit for your AS based on what you've told me. For money, SkatteFUNN is the safest bet. The EIC Accelerator is the bigger option, and it has accepted defence and dual-use companies since June 2026.

Would you set up a Swedish AB for the Linköping team? Swedish programmes need a company registered in Sweden, and your contractors' own firms can't apply for you. A Swedish AB would open Vinnova, Almi and Sweden AI Factory.

● Sigma2 KI-fabrikken (GPU): GPU access and help with models for Norwegian companies. It publishes no price or hours. Deciding factor: whether a startup pays. 5 requirements, 3 unclear points.
● SkatteFUNN (money): 19% of approved R&D costs, on up to NOK 25 000 000 of costs a year. You get it as cash if you make a loss. Deciding factor: one R&D project kept separate from normal work. The 1 September 2026 date for a guaranteed decision this year has passed. 8 requirements, 2 unclear points.
◐ EIC Accelerator (money): a grant below EUR 2 500 000, with equity as an option. Deciding factor: technology at TRL 6–8, and a short proposal comes first. 6 requirements, 2 unclear points.

More options: Innovasjon Norge startup grants 1 and 2 (they depend on company age). EuroHPC Playground gives free GPU hours, but only for civilian work, so defence training likely won't qualify. Norrsken Evolve invests for equity.

Which one do you want to look at? Then you get the full list of what the application needs.

## Founder, turn 2 (simulated)
Not sure about setting up a Swedish AB. Let's look at Sigma2 KI-fabrikken first, tell me more.

## Assistant, turn 2
```
●  Sigma2 KI-fabrikken (Norwegian AI Factory)
●  in Norway
→  KI-fabrikken publishes no price or hours. Industry pays NOK 35 per GPU hour on national systems, or NOK 5.35 per GPU hour on LUMI (category C).
```

KI-fabrikken is Norway's national AI hub. It gives Norwegian companies GPU access, expert advice and onboarding. Your Trondheim AS fits, but expect to pay for the hours unless Sigma2 tells you otherwise.

**Before you apply**
- To start, use the contact form on the KI-fabrikken page (name, email, phone, message, consent) or email contact@sigma2.no.
- For a national allocation, you log in to metacenter.no with Feide or an OpenIdP account.
- The project leader must be employed by the AS. Your Linköping contractors can be users, but they can't lead the project.
- Your price group is C (commercial and industry): NOK 35 per GPU hour on national systems, NOK 5.35 on LUMI. The rate also depends on the GPU generation. You get one invoice a year.
- If you reserve capacity, you pay for the reserved time even when you don't use it.
- Backup is not included in the prices. That matters for 40 TB of sensor data.
- There are two allocation calls a year, with deadlines usually in February and August. Those dates come from Sigma2's allocation pages, which I didn't re-check today. Outside the calls, requests take a few working days if there is capacity.
- EU-funded projects can be exempt from these prices. To qualify, you must talk to Sigma2 before you submit the EU application, fill in their EU form, and stay within what the EU funding covers.

**Unclear**
- The page doesn't say whether a startup gets AI Factory services free or pays the category C price. Ask contact@sigma2.no.
- Nothing on the page covers defence work, export-controlled data or classified sensor data on shared systems, including LUMI, which sits in Finland. Ask Sigma2 before you move any data.
- The page names no route other than the contact form. A metacenter.no allocation is a separate, paid route. Ask Sigma2 which one you'd use.
- The exact deadline for the next call isn't published. Ask Sigma2.
- Storage prices for a dataset of this size aren't shown. Ask Sigma2.

**Next step:** Fill in the contact form at https://www.sigma2.no/nb/ki-fabrikken#toc-heading--ta-kontakt. Describe the AS, the training workload, the 40 TB of data and that it is for defence. Sigma2 will suggest a service and an access route. You click submit yourself.

Do you want to see the next option, SkatteFUNN or the EIC Accelerator, or answer an open question first?
