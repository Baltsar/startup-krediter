## Assistant, turn 1
On what we know, your best fit today is SkatteFUNN. It gives back 19% of R&D costs, on up to NOK 25M of costs a year, and pays it out in cash if you run at a loss. Counter-drone is not excluded on the call page.

How old is the AS, in years?

● SkatteFUNN: up to NOK 4.75M a year as a tax credit or cash payout. Rolling intake. Decides: whether the project counts as R&D, separate from normal operations. 8 requirements in the application, 2 unclear points.
● Sigma2 KI-fabrikken (Norwegian AI Factory): GPU access and help moving your training onto HPC. No price or hour limits published, so you agree them by contact. 5 requirements, 3 unclear points.
◐ Innovasjon Norge Startup grant 1: up to NOK 150 000, applications any time. Decides: the company must be under five years old. Only invoices from outside suppliers count. Invoices from related parties don't, so check whether your Swedish devs' firms count as related. 7 requirements, 3 unclear points.

SkatteFUNN and the grant are money. Sigma2 is GPU.

The EU's defence startup accelerator (EUDIS Business Accelerator) is open to Norway and gives a EUR 120 000 voucher. Both 2026 rounds are closed and no next date is published.

More options: EuroHPC Playground (free GPU hours), EIC Accelerator (next deadline 4 November 2026; it has said it will invest in defence and dual-use), Startup grant 2, Eureka Innowwide.

Which one do you want to look at? Then you get the full list of what the application needs.
