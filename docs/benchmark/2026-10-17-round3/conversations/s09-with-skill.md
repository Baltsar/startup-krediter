This is Business Finland's funding for development and pilot projects. It is paid as a loan; grants are only for research projects. On what we know, it fits a Finnish Oy with your team, and Business Finland decides each case on its own judgment.

**The loan, from today's page:** interest is 3 percentage points below the base rate, with a 1% minimum. There is usually no collateral. The term is 7 or 10 years, and the first 3 or 5 years have no repayments. 30% can be paid in advance. In exceptional cases, if the project doesn't reach its results, some or all of the loan can be turned into a grant.

**Before you apply**
- Order matters. If the company is under five years old and has never had Business Finland funding, apply for the Sprint grant first. Taking this loan first blocks Sprint.
- Suomi.fi e-identification. Only a Trade Register representative with sole signing right, or someone holding the Suomi.fi mandate "Applying for corporate financing", can create the application.
- A project plan on Business Finland's template, plus the company background information form.
- An income statement and balance sheet from your accounts, at most two months old. You also need a monthly cash flow forecast for the project and an annual profit and funding forecast, on the e-service Excel templates.
- A team of at least two people in Finland, one of them full-time. You also need a named contact person and a project leader who is employed by the company or holds a responsible role.
- A credible funding plan for the whole project, and money to run the rest of the business meanwhile. You pay costs first. After the advance, funding is paid against reports, and the last 20% of the loan comes only after the final report is approved.
- No binding purchases before the application is pending. Costs count from that point at the earliest.
- Production, marketing, sales and fundraising costs are not eligible. Neither are ready-made solutions or minor improvements.
- Show that the funding changes the project: its scope, its speed or your total R&D spending. It must also have one further effect, such as a leap in ambition. Projects that would go ahead anyway are not funded.
- AWS and other cloud costs count as equipment rental. Only the share used in the project is eligible.
- General conditions:
  - The company is in the prepayment register.
  - Its beneficial-ownership notice and audited financial statements are filed with the Finnish Patent and Registration Office.
  - There are no overdue taxes without a payment plan.
  - VAT, the employer register and statutory employer insurance are in order.
  - There are no arrears on earlier Tekes or Business Finland loans.
- The company must not be a "firm in difficulty":
  - From three years old, it must not have lost more than half its share capital to accumulated losses.
  - Under three years old, only bankruptcy or insolvency counts.
  - This is checked at the time of the decision, for the whole group.
- After a decision you need:
  - project accounting with working-time tracking
  - an independent auditor's report covering the whole project, filed with the final report
  - a promissory note signed with the State Treasury within six months
  - a report of any income the project's results bring in
