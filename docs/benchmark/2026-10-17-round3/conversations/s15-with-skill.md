
AWS Activate gives you AWS credits that can also pay for Claude if you call it through Amazon Bedrock. On what you've told me it fits a pre-seed company. Your 2025 round decides two things: whether you can get credits now, and which tier you get.

**Before you apply**
- **Login and email:** an AWS Builder ID verified by email, and an Activate profile with a business email on your own domain. Free email providers are rejected, and the domain must match the email on your AWS account.
- **AWS account:** it must be on a paid plan, because free plans don't qualify. Link it to the Builder ID with root or admin access.
- **Website:** a working company website or public profile. AWS reads public information from it.
- **Round date:** your most recent funding round must be less than 12 months old when you apply (AWS FAQ). A round that closed before 17 October 2025 is already older than that. Rounds beyond Series A, and private equity rounds, are declined.
- **Which tier:**
  - Founders (1,000 USD, up to 5,000 USD) is for bootstrapped or self-funded startups. The FAQ says startups not tied to an Activate Provider may also be eligible.
  - The form asks your funding stage and the date of your latest round, so your SEK 4 million round will show.
  - If one of your investors or an accelerator is an Activate Provider, use Portfolio (up to 200,000 USD). Ask them for an email invitation (approved in 3 to 5 days) or their Org ID (7 to 10 days).
- **Portfolio confirmation:** the provider must confirm you by answering AWS's verification email, or you are declined after 30 days. Apply with the same email the invitation went to. Invitations expire after 30 days, and each Org ID works only once.
- **The form:** it asks for product, target market, traction, funding stage and date of the latest round. You can't edit it after submitting, only cancel and apply again.
- **ID check:** if AWS asks, you verify your identity with an ID document through a link they email you. If you decline, the application is rejected.
- **Paying for Claude:** to spend the credits on Claude, call it through Amazon Bedrock, not Anthropic's own API.
- **What credits don't cover:** upfront fees for Savings Plans or Reserved Instances. You also can't pass the credits on to your customers.
- **Expiry:** credits expire on a date shown in the AWS Billing console, and AWS does not extend it.
- **Government-funded companies:** the Activate terms exclude government entities, including those "substantially funded by" a government.

**Unclear**
- **Review time:** the credits page says 5 to 10 business days, the FAQ says 7 to 10. Ask AWS Activate through its Contact Activate form.
- **Founders amount:** it starts at 1,000 USD, and "select participants" get up to 5,000 USD, with no criteria given. Portfolio amounts depend on the provider's offer. Ask your investor, or AWS Activate.
- **12-month rule:** it's unclear whether it also applies to the Founders tier. The FAQ says "Activate Credits" without naming a tier. Ask AWS Activate.
- **Government funding:** "substantially funded by" a government is not defined. If much of your money came from public sources, such as a hospital, region or university holding company, ask AWS Activate before applying.
