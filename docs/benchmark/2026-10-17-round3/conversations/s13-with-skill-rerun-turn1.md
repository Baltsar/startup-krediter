## Assistant, turn 1
Yes, it's open, though the grant is a lump sum below EUR 2.5 million, beside EUR 1–10 million investment. Short applications go in any time, batched monthly, feedback in about 4–6 weeks. The last 2026 full-application cut-off is 4 November, so yours goes to 2027; check the portal for dates.

No one gives odds: experts screen the short application, score the full one, and the top-ranked meet a jury. On what we know you fit, if the pilot line is TRL 6–8.

Energimyndigheten allows stacking: its spring 2026 call text requires declaring Horizon Europe funding for the same costs and caps total aid on them. Whether EIC money can be your co-financing isn't stated; ask Energimyndigheten. Pilot och demonstration 2026 closed 1 June, no next round announced.
