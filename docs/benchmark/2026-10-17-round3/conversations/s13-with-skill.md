## Founder, turn 1
Hi! We're a battery materials startup in Västerås, AB founded 2023, 11 people. We want to apply to the EIC Accelerator for the full €2.5M grant plus the equity part to build our pilot line, total need is about €9M. Is the short application open right now and what are our chances? Also can we stack it with Swedish Energy Agency money?

## Assistant, turn 1
Correction first: the EIC grant is a lump sum below EUR 2.5 million, not the full 2.5. It pays for TRL 6–8 work done within 24 months. The equity part is EUR 1–10 million, so on what we know a EUR 9 million plan fits its ranges. The short application is open at any time. The next batch is Tuesday 3 November 2026, 17:00 Brussels time.

Nobody can give you odds. You get a GO or NO GO in about 4–6 weeks. After that the top-scored full proposals meet the EIC Jury, which decides. The last 2026 full-application date (4 November) comes before any GO, and the 2027 dates aren't published yet.

◐ EIC Accelerator: grant below EUR 2.5 million plus a EUR 1–10 million investment in exchange for an ownership stake. Decides: the GO, then the jury interview. 6 requirements in the application, 2 unclear points.

Stacking: yes, with limits. Energimyndigheten's call text for Verifiering av innovationer med kund (max SEK 3 million) sets three rules:
- You must declare Horizon Europe funding for the same costs.
- Total public aid on those costs must stay under the EU ceilings.
- It doesn't accept "other public funds" as your own share.

That call is planned to reopen in October, with no day given. Pilot och demonstration (projects from SEK 10 million) closed on 1 June 2026, and no next round is published.

## Assistant, turn 2
Thanks, that settles the last open point. At about EUR 0.19 million turnover you're well inside the SME limit, so on what we know the EIC Accelerator fits.

```
●  EIC Accelerator
●  a company, or no company yet
●  fewer than 250 employees
●  turnover at most 50 MEUR, or balance sheet at most 43 MEUR
→  Grant component below EUR 2.5 million (lump sum); investment component EUR 1-10 million
```

The EIC Accelerator gives one company a grant for development and demonstration work (TRL 6–8) plus an EIC Fund equity investment for scale-up.

**Before you apply**
1. **Portal account:** set up EU Login and get a validated 9-digit PIC (company ID) on the EU Funding and Tenders portal.
2. **Step 1, short application:**
   - a 12-page form, which includes your estimated costs and why you need EU support;
   - a pitch deck of at most 10 slides, as PDF;
   - a video of at most 3 minutes with up to three core team members (any EU language).

   Four experts review it, and you need a GO from at least three. You can submit at any time. The next batch is Tuesday 3 November 2026 at 17:00 Brussels time, and results take about 4–6 weeks.
3. **Technology level:** all of TRL 5 (validated in a relevant environment) must already be done. The grant can't pay for anything above TRL 8, so commercial production has to sit on the investment side.
4. **Grant terms:** a lump sum covering at most 70% of eligible TRL 6–8 costs, and you fund the rest. The cap is EUR 2 499 999. In a grant-plus-equity application, EUR 2.5 million or more is possible "only in exceptional cases". Each work package is paid by either the grant or the investment, not both.
5. **Equity terms:** EUR 1–10 million as equity or a convertible, for at most 25% of the voting shares.
   - The EIC Fund wants at least as much money from other investors as it puts in, and it uses their valuation.
   - The investment must be made within 12 months after the grant ends.
   - You must show that private investors won't fund this alone yet.
6. **Step 2, full application:** only after a GO. You will need:
   - a 20-page form;
   - a pitch deck (no slide limit, but you get 10 minutes to present it);
   - a 3-minute video;
   - a lump-sum budget table;
   - an implementation plan of at most 10 pages;
   - a financial plan showing the equity you need;
   - a freedom-to-operate analysis, or a note of at most 2 pages;
   - letters of intent, using the portal's template;
   - links to key CVs.

   You also answer security questions and agree to share your data with the EIC Fund.
7. **Open call or Challenge:** at Step 2 you choose between the open call and a themed Challenge. One of the 2026 Challenges is "Advanced Materials for Renewable Energy and Energy Storage Systems". The EIC won't move a proposal from one to the other.
8. **Step 2 deadline:** submit before a batching date, at 17:00 Brussels time and ideally 48 hours early. The last 2026 date is 4 November 2026, which comes before you could get a GO. 2027 dates will be set in the 2027 Work Programme.
9. **Expert interview:** an online interview with a technology expert about 3–4 weeks after the batch, with at most 3 people and no consultants. Results come 8–9 weeks after the batch.
10. **Step 3, Jury interview:** 45 minutes in English, in Brussels or remote: a 10-minute pitch, then questions.
    - Up to three people can attend: your staff, board members or investors. No consultants.
    - The result comes about 3 weeks later, and travel costs aren't reimbursed.
11. **Application limits:** one application at a time. After three rejections at any stage you can't apply again before the end of 2027. The rule about moving to the EU only applies to non-EU applicants.

**Unclear**
- **2027 full-application dates:** not published yet. Ask your National Contact Point (the link is on the EIC page) or check the portal topic in a browser.
- **What counts as an "exceptional case"** for a grant of EUR 2.5 million or more: not defined. Ask your National Contact Point.
- **Whether your materials fit the energy-storage Challenge:** its text names energy generation and "mid-long energy storage", so it may not cover your application. Ask your National Contact Point before you choose.
