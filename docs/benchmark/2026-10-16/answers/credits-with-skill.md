AWS Activate gives promotional AWS credits. It fits a pre-seed Swedish AB building on Anthropic only if you move your Claude calls to Amazon Bedrock. Your angel round decides which tier you use.

**Before you apply**
- An AWS Builder ID verified by email, and an Activate profile. The credits page asks for a business email on your own domain. The FAQ (read today) says to create the Builder ID with your personal email. Use your company-domain email to be safe.
- An AWS account on a paid account plan, linked to that Builder ID with root or admin access. Free plans are not eligible.
- A working company website. The FAQ says "website or public profile", and AWS judges you on public information.
- The form asks for product, target market, traction, funding stage and the date of your latest round. Your angel round will show.
- The Founders tier is for bootstrapped or self-funded startups. With outside funding, ask your angels whether any of them is an Activate Provider, and if so apply on the Portfolio tier.
- For the Portfolio tier, you need an Org ID or email invitation from your provider, and your latest round must be within the last 12 months.
- For the Portfolio tier, the provider must answer AWS's verification email. If they don't, the application is declined.
- AWS may ask you to verify your identity with an ID document. Saying no means rejection.
- An application cannot be edited after you submit it. You can only cancel through AWS and apply again, and you must first unlink the account ID.
- Credits also pay for third-party models on Amazon Bedrock, including Claude. They do not cover other AWS Marketplace charges, Route 53 domain registration, or upfront Savings Plan or Reserved Instance fees.
- Credits expire. You see the date in the AWS Billing console after approval, not before.

**Unclear**
- Whether an angel-funded startup whose angels are not Activate Providers can still use the Founders tier. The credits page says self-funded, but the FAQ says the program is open to "bootstrapped, self-funded, and funded startups". Ask AWS Activate through the contact page.
- Review time: 5 to 10 business days on the credits page, 7 to 10 in the FAQ. Ask AWS Activate if timing matters.
- The Founders tier says select startups may get up to 5 000 USD but gives no criteria. Portfolio amounts depend on the provider and are not published. Ask AWS Activate, or your provider.
- The FAQ answer to "Why wasn't my credits application approved?" is a broken placeholder. Ask AWS Activate support.
