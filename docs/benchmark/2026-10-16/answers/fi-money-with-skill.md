Sprint is a de minimis grant from Business Finland. It covers 75% of an R&D project costing €100,000–133,334, for startups under five years old that have never had Business Finland funding. On size and form you qualify. The real test is the money below.

**Before you apply**
- Register in My Business Finland and fill in the company's background information.
- Book and hold a meeting with a Business Finland expert. You can submit the application only after that meeting.
- Log in to the Funding e-service with Suomi.fi. The application must be created by a Trade Register representative with sole signing right, or by someone with the Suomi.fi mandate "Applying for corporate financing".
- Attachments:
  - a project plan on the Sprint template
  - your list of shareholders
  - an account-level income statement and balance sheet from your accounting system, no older than two months, with the income statement covering the period from the start of the current fiscal year
  - optionally, a pitch deck
- At least €50,000 of equity financing on the balance sheet.
- Net financial assets (cash plus short-term receivables minus short-term liabilities) of at least €63,334. For a company under five years old that is the project cost minus the advance. On top of that, you need enough money to cover the company's other costs during the project.
- A committed team of at least two people working in Finland, at least one of them full time. Your two full-time co-founders meet this.
- At least two-thirds of the project cost must be R&D, and the project must include customer and market research.
- Sign no binding purchase agreements before you submit. Any purchase of €60,000 or more must be tendered as a public procurement.
- No earlier Business Finland funding of any kind: Sprint, Tempo, R&D funding, Young Innovative Companies or Deep Tech Accelerator. Apply for Sprint before any Business Finland loan.
- No earlier public funding for the same solution, and your de minimis quota must not be used up. Count any other de minimis aid you have received.
- The company must not be insolvent or in bankruptcy or restructuring proceedings. You are under three years old, so the "lost half of share capital" test does not apply to you yet.
- The company must be in the prepayment register.
- It must have filed its beneficial-ownership notice with the Trade Register.
- It must have filed its financial statements with the Finnish Patent and Registration Office, audited where the Auditing Act requires it.
- No overdue taxes without a payment arrangement, and no gaps in tax returns, VAT or employer register duties, or employer insurance.
- How the money arrives: 70% as an advance, because you are under five years old and no company in your group is older. You get the rest only after the project ends, so you pay those costs yourself first.
