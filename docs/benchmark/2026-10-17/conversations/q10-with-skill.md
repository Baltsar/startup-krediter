Yes. As a Chalmers postdoc, your best fit is probably Chalmers' own Entrepreneurial Fellowship. It pays you to develop the result for a year, and you don't have to start a company first. The next best is Almi's verification grant, which requires a Swedish personnummer.

Check this with Migrationsverket before you register a company or take a salary from one.
