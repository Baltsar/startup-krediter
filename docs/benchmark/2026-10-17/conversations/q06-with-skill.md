## Founder, turn 1
anyone know where to get free gpu hours? we're training a vision model for a warehouse picking robot and our runpod bill is getting silly, like 2-3k a month. small team, no funding yet but we have a working prototype

## Assistant, turn 1
Happy to help. GPU hours do exist for this. Free public compute for AI companies and vendor credit programs both fit a vision model like yours. Which ones you can use depends mostly on where your company is registered.

Which country is your company registered in, or where will you register it?

## Founder, turn 2 (simulated)
sweden, we're an AB in gothenburg

## Assistant, turn 2
Good, that helps. EuroHPC Playground fits best on what we know. It gives a Swedish AB 5 000 free GPU hours for training. What decides it is how long your project lead stays employed.

Will your project lead be employed until at least three months after the GPU time ends?

◐ EuroHPC Playground: 5 000 GPU hours free, 1 to 3 months, on one EU supercomputer. Open all year, first come, first served. Decides it: the project lead's employment. 11 things the application asks for, 3 unclear points.
● Sweden AI Factory: free help choosing the right EU call and checking your application. No hours of its own until early 2027. 4 things to have ready, 4 unclear points.
● Runpod Startup Program: 1 000 USD in Runpod credits. Decides it: it is mainly for new customers, and you already use Runpod. Selective. 5 things to have ready, 3 unclear points.

The first two are free compute. Runpod is credits on the bill you already pay.

More options: NVIDIA Inception.

Which one do you want to look at? Then you get the full list of what the application asks for.
